## Outcome

Publish a finite English guide cluster that explains RITUVIA's original secular ritual and
reflection loop without publishing a regional spiritual tradition, physical fire or smoke
instructions, efficacy claims, private content, or paid-object superiority.

## Scope

intention-to-small-action guide; a private post-ritual reflection guide; a voluntary revisit
guide; exact binding to the approved ritual catalog; original answer-first editorial copy; source,
rights, review, safety, and indexing metadata; strict server parsing; semantic static components;
production-only metadata; shared editorial registration; exact active routes and sitemap;
responsive/accessibility styles; focused build, contract, and browser tests.

Excluded: regional or religious traditions; physical candle, incense, smoke, breathing, health, or
crisis instructions; occasion/relationship/astrological ritual pages; efficacy, manifestation, or
outcome claims; personalized or keyword-substitution routes; user input; public journal examples;
AI retrieval; paid objects; another locale; database, provider, payment, deployment, DNS, or public
launch.

## Risks and rollback

The highest risks are presenting a RITUVIA product exercise as a historical or religious ritual,
implying virtual actions alter external outcomes, publishing physical fire/smoke or health advice,
exposing private reflection text, and multiplying thin occasion or intention pages.

The publication therefore names the method as RITUVIA original secular reflection, describes only
virtual product behavior, keeps all examples synthetic and agency-based, and denies AI retrieval
and personalization. Rollback removes its shared editorial and public-route registrations, returns
metadata to noindex, and retains the approved artifact and D-082 for audit; no database or customer
data rollback is required.
