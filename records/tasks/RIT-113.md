# RIT-113: Programmatic public-page inventory and quality gate

- Backlog item: RIT-113
- Related records: D-076, D-080, D-083
- Owner approval: Not required for the internal quality gate; any new public content, locale,
  indexing scope, policy, deployment, DNS change, or public launch remains separately owner-gated

The queue state, priority, milestone, dependencies, and owner gates remain canonical in
[BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).

## Outcome

Create one authoritative inventory and fail-closed quality gate for every approved public route so
only source-bound, reviewed, unique, substantive, non-cannibalizing pages can enter canonical,
robots, sitemap, and indexing output.

## Scope

Included: audit the complete active public inventory; stable route and content identity; locale,
content family, user intent, source/editorial record, canonical path, review/freshness, structured
content-shape, internal-link, and uniqueness evidence; duplicate, near-duplicate, thin,
cannibalization, keyword-substitution, private, personalized, and unreviewed denial; deterministic
quality findings; focused mutation tests; CI/evidence integration; documentation.

Excluded: writing a new content cluster; adding a locale, country, tradition, product, policy, or
indexable route; search-console credentials; analytics ingestion; AI-generated publication;
payment, database, deployment, DNS, public launch, or automated content removal.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every active public route maps to one stable inventory record with exact route, locale,
      content family, user intent, editorial/source authority, review, and canonical evidence.
- [x] The inventory rejects unknown, duplicate, private, personalized, query-bearing, fragment,
      framework-representation, or unapproved routes.
- [x] Deterministic quality checks identify exact duplicates, bounded near-duplicates, thin pages,
      template-only substitution, canonical collision, and same-intent cannibalization without
      sending page content or sensitive terms to an external provider.
- [x] Canonical, robots, and sitemap publication fails closed when a route lacks current passing
      inventory evidence.
- [x] Existing finite numerology, astrology, Tarot, ritual/reflection, and core-page inventories
      pass without widening their approved route sets.
- [x] Focused typecheck, lint, formatting, unit/contract tests, architecture, records, generated
      evidence, and secret checks pass.

## Verification evidence

- D-083 records one atomic source-bound authorization for exactly 45 approved English routes:
  four core pages, five numerology pages, five astrology pages, 25 Tarot pages, and six
  ritual/reflection pages across five locale/content sitemap shards.
- `pnpm check:public-pages` recomputes every record from current route, copy, editorial artifact,
  review, source digest, internal-link, intent, structure, and uniqueness evidence. The checked-in
  inventory has 45 unique content digests and 45 unique intents; its highest reviewed nearest-page
  similarity remains below the template-substitution threshold.
- Mutation tests cover missing coverage, source/review expiry, canonical/private drift, exact and
  near duplicates, short-page containment, template substitution, thin content, same-intent
  cannibalization, unsafe exposure, and broken internal links.
- Canonical/hreflang metadata, robots, sitemap, the non-restricted configuration boundary, and the
  Web build policy consume the same inventory and fail the complete crawl surface closed on any
  missing or stale record.
- Eighteen affected test files pass 168 tests. Strict content and Web typechecks, focused ESLint and
  formatting, CI contract, architecture, records, generated evidence, and secret checks pass.
  The optimized Next.js build generates 60 pages and the public build policy validates all 45
  routes.
- The full workspace build proceeds through all 16 package builds and the complete public-page
  policy, then remains blocked by the pre-existing private three-card JavaScript aggregate
  (`247950` bytes against a `237568`-byte limit). RIT-113 does not modify that private client slice
  or raise its budget; this unrelated release issue remains visible rather than being silenced.

## Risks and rollback

The quality gate remains deterministic rather than semantic authority. Its fielded family shapes,
three-token Jaccard, five-token containment, exact intent ownership, and conservative thresholds
avoid treating word count as sufficient, while human cultural, source, legal, and editorial review
remain independent. New legitimate content patterns may require a reviewed policy-version change
rather than a threshold bypass.

Rollback removes the new inventory/gate integration and restores the current D-076 route registry
as the sole crawl source while preserving test evidence. No database or customer data rollback is
required.
