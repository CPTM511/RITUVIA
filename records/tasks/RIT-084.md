## Outcome

RIT-084 publishes a bounded English education cluster at `/en/numerology` with four substantive
method guides for Life Path, Birthday Number, Personal Year, and master numbers. Every page
renders useful static HTML with transparent formulas, synthetic examples, strict limits,
reflection questions, source/review context, canonical metadata, breadcrumbs, JSON-LD, sitemap
timestamps, and contextual links to safety, privacy, sibling guides, and the separate private
calculator.

The twelve owner-approved number profiles remain source records for interpretation and editorial
reuse. They are not public routes, are absent from robots and sitemap allowlists, and cannot become
thin keyword-substitution doorway pages.

## Scope

Included: exact locale-prefixed route inventory; static generation; unique title, description,
canonical, Open Graph, `en`/`x-default` alternates; `CollectionPage`, `Article`, and
`BreadcrumbList` structured data; substantive source-backed English copy; exact D-064 formulas;
synthetic ordinary and `11`/`22`/`33` examples; explicit non-scientific and non-predictive
boundaries; production-only indexing; sitemap `lastmod`; calculator backlink; mobile,
reduced-motion, dark-mode, forced-colors, keyboard, touch-target, contrast, and axe coverage; and a
dedicated production-artifact browser verifier.

Excluded: public number-profile routes, name numerology, another locale or tradition, private
birth-data indexing, FAQ/review schema, public user-generated content, personalized AI output,
paid activation, deployment, DNS, and public launch.

## Risks and rollback

Search quality after public launch requires real crawl/index monitoring and human review; local
checks cannot prove ranking or prevent all future content drift. The pages are English-only and
must not be machine-translated into an unreviewed locale. Production publication remains an owner
deployment gate.

Rollback removes the five exact public route records, pages, metadata, browser verifier, and
calculator backlink. The private deterministic calculator, D-064 method, and D-065 approved
non-indexed interpretation corpus can remain safe-off. There is no migration, secret, provider
call, payment action, remote state, deployment, or customer data to reverse.
