- Owner approval: Not required; Arabic remains inactive and no production gate is crossed

The queue state, priority, milestone, dependencies, and owner gates remain canonical in

## Outcome

RITUVIA now has locale-derived text direction, test-only `en-XA` and `ar-XB` pseudolocales,
ICU-AST-preserving expansion, structural bidi isolation for current mixed-direction values, and a
repository RTL policy enforced by CI. The production-artifact accessibility matrix covers all
fourteen approved public routes plus the private intake and Tarot pages in expanded and RTL states.

English remains the sole active runtime locale. No Arabic route, catalog, email, share card,
country, support commitment, or production release is created.

## Scope

Included: canonical locale direction metadata; exact test pseudolocale inventory; ICU argument,
plural, select, tag, URL, email, local-route, and brand preservation; forty-percent expansion;
logical directional CSS; icon mirroring; `dir="auto"` user input and user-content isolation;
explicit LTR formulas, dates, time inputs, engine identifiers, and astrology chart direction;
responsive time fields; production-import denial; full current Web message traversal; English
reminder-copy pseudolocale evidence; private social-output denial; CI and production-artifact
browser gates; and repair of the stale public-shell artifact policy exposed by this work.

Excluded: Arabic publication; real Arabic translation; Arabic fonts and linguistic review;
localized routing, canonical, hreflang, sitemap, or redirects; localized reminder templates or
delivery; localized share-card generation; cross-browser release matrix; production deployment;
DNS; and public launch. Those remain with RIT-103, RIT-104, RIT-105/RIT-106, RIT-115, and release
tasks.

## Approval outcome

## Risks and rollback

Pseudolocalization cannot establish Arabic linguistic quality, font quality, cultural integrity,
legal sufficiency, or real SSR/hydration behavior. Chromium evidence does not replace the future
cross-browser locale release matrix. Actual email and share-card RTL remain unimplemented rather
than falsely accepted.

Rollback removes `@rituvia/i18n/testing`, the RTL policy and CI step, structural direction markup,
and expanded accessibility inventory. The public home remains static to satisfy its canonical
build contract. No migration, customer data, production state, secret, provider, payment, remote
deployment, or irreversible action requires reversal.
