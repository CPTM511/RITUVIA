- Backlog item: RIT-095
- Related records: D-070

The queue state, priority, milestone, dependencies, and owner gates remain canonical in

## Outcome

The repository has a provider-neutral, safe-off English natal interpretation boundary that
reparses authoritative RIT-093 facts, independently recomputes every major aspect, minimizes model
context to exact fact references and bounded provenance, preserves approximate-time uncertainty,
binds a single-use candidate by digest and input identity, rejects deterministic or safety drift,
requires an independently authorized semantic reviewer, and supplies a deterministic safe
replacement plus a checksum-bound zero-tolerance synthetic evaluation gate.

## Scope

Included: strict exact/approximate available-fact admission; unavailable-fact fail-closed behavior;
aspect recomputation from authoritative placement longitudes; minimized placement/aspect
references; bounded method, engine, and source provenance; strict English
content/prompt/fallback schemas; separate SHA-256 integrity and server authority; exact fact order,
source, uncertainty, and schema binding; deterministic label and numeric prose exclusion;
single-use pending candidates; digest and input-identity binding; whole-output safety checks;
independent semantic review; deterministic safe replacement; privacy-safe metadata; and a fixed
synthetic release evaluator integrated into the combined AI gate.

Excluded: production interpretation content, a provider or reviewer model, provider SDK/key/network
request, paid inference, Credits, database persistence, API, natal UI prose, calculation mutation,
another locale, production activation, deployment, and public launch.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Exact facts are strictly reparsed and all persisted aspects are independently recomputed from
      placement longitudes before interpretation artifacts are accepted.
- [x] Unknown-time, engine-unavailable, and untrusted-output facts stop before artifact authority
      or model work; approximate-time facts preserve the exact window while exposing no houses,
      angles, or aspects.
- [x] Provider context excludes birth date, time, place, coordinates, profile revision, Julian day,
      exact degrees, house cusps, questions, journals, identity, and payment data.
- [x] Candidate output cannot add, remove, reorder, restate, or narrate a placement, aspect, degree,
      house, or uncertainty fact; structured references remain server-authoritative.
- [x] Content, prompt, and fallback artifacts require exact independent integrity and authority;
      candidates are digest-bound, input-bound, and single-use.
- [x] Certainty, professional advice, mind-reading, dependency, paid efficacy, persecution,
      self-harm, injection, fixed-personality, fact narration, numeric degrees, markup, bidi,
      zero-width, and wrong-script output use the approved deterministic replacement.
- [x] Independent semantic review, safe negation, malformed output, reviewer uncertainty, trust
      denial, source binding, locale, and privacy-safe metadata tests pass.
- [x] The fixed release evaluator requires zero critical, metric, safe-control, missing, or
      unexpected failures and records zero external requests and zero paid calls.

## Risks and rollback

The synthetic content and reviewer prove contract behavior, not production meaning quality,
cultural review, provider behavior, or live-model safety. Production content, provider/model,
privacy settings, cost/latency controls, persistence, Credits, UI activation, deployment, and
public release remain separate work and owner gates.

Rollback removes the astrology interpretation module, focused fixtures/evaluator, exports, and AI
gate registration. It does not alter RIT-093 facts, encrypted calculations, the private RIT-094
viewer, native archives, database migrations, feature flags, or deployed behavior.
