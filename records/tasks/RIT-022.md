# RIT-022: Versioned tarot deck, spread, and content schema

- Backlog item: RIT-022
- Related records: D-031

The queue state, priority, milestone, dependencies, and owner gates remain canonical in
[BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).

## Outcome

The repository can strictly validate a versioned English tarot content snapshot, source/rights
graph, deck/card identities, both orientations, one-card and ordered three-card spreads, and
structural publication eligibility without copying unlicensed material or creating a runtime
content service.

## Scope

Included: a pure `@rituvia/divination` package; exact V1 unknown-input contracts; independent source,
rights, deck, spread, artwork, content, translation, editorial, and supersession versions; strict
cross-reference/tradition checks; deep immutability; explicit dated structural eligibility; a
three-card/six-orientation-content synthetic English fixture; one-card and
Situation/Action/Possibility spread definitions; tests; compiled export verification; and durable
decision/status evidence.

Excluded: entropy or drawing, replacement/redraw rules, idempotency, a reading service or API,
database persistence or migration, UI, real traditional tarot content, production artwork, AI
retrieval or interpretation, indexing, another locale, publication, activation, and deployment.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Deck, card identity, orientation content, spread positions, source, rights, editorial,
      translation, and exact version references are separate, strict, immutable, and validated.
- [x] The synthetic fixture has three original neutral cards, upright/reversed complete content,
      one-card and ordered three-card spreads, no copied art/text, and explicit internal-only rights.
- [x] Draft, incomplete, unsafe, stale, expired, revoked, cross-tradition, missing-rights, or
      publication-disallowed content fails closed with stable non-content-bearing reason/error codes.
- [x] The pure eligibility assessor accepts only explicit `asOf` input and cannot read files, time,
      environment, network, database, AI, UI, analytics, or perform publication.
- [x] Focused tests, lint, format, strict type checking, architecture, full unit/integration, and
      production build gates pass; no UI/E2E behavior or migration is applicable.
- [x] Backlog, project status, decision/task records, record index, compiled manual, and checksums
      reflect the final verified slice.

`pnpm check` passes on Node.js 24.18.0 and pnpm 11.13.1: generated/record/migration/secret

## Risks and rollback

The fixture proves contracts, not a production content corpus. Its small size and original secular
labels avoid unverified tradition and rights claims, but cannot support a useful public reading. A
real deck requires independently verified text/art permissions, complete ten-theme content,
cultural and safety review, attribution, artwork accessibility, and applicable owner gates. The
prohibited-claim lexicon is a deterministic guard, not a substitute for human review. Structural
eligibility also cannot prove that an external approval reference is authentic; a future authorized
publishing workflow must resolve and audit it.

There is no migration, public route, feature flag, runtime consumer, retained data, or deployment.
Roll back by removing the package/content snapshot and restoring the workspace/build verifier and
lockfile. Historical content must be superseded through new exact versions rather than silently
edited once consumers persist version references.
