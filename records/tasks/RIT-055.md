# RIT-055: Consent and AI-Personalization Controls

- Backlog item: RIT-055
- Related records: D-029, D-051, D-062

The queue state, priority, milestone, dependencies, and owner gates remain canonical in
[BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).

## Outcome

Provide one signed-in, append-only, purpose/version consent authority whose committed withdrawal
is visible across sessions and is reread at every sensitive data-flow boundary, while keeping
analytics, provider AI, model training, marketing, and notification delivery safe-off.

## Scope

Included: independent analytics, AI-personalization, and model-improvement purpose registries;
strict mutation parsing; account-owned append-only database records; exact replay/conflict
semantics; same-account serialization; bounded fail-closed evaluation; least-privilege runtime
access; private GET/POST API; accessible account controls; explicit-selected-excerpt AI gate;
privacy export; and focused domain/database/API/UI/browser evidence.

Excluded: production analytics sink/outbox/beacon/warehouse; marketing or service-notification
delivery; real-user model training/evaluation; AI-provider calls with private content; queue
cancellation/Credit settlement for paid Deep Readings; final legal notice or retention policy;
production migration; deployment; public launch; and golden screenshot changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Analytics, AI personalization, and model improvement use separate exact purpose/notice
      versions; one grant never implies another or service-notification/marketing permission.
- [x] Account consent is append-only, idempotent, sequential, account-scoped, and unavailable to
      runtime update/delete.
- [x] Absence, denial, withdrawal, stale version, malformed/oversized history, or inactive session
      fails closed.
- [x] A committed withdrawal is immediately visible to every account session and every new
      selected-excerpt data-flow check; no login/page-load consent cache is authoritative.
- [x] Strict private API and accessible UI expose loading, retry, failure, saved, and safe-off
      states without optimistic success.
- [x] Privacy export includes account and anonymous consent evidence; deletion retains consent
      history under the existing policy.
- [x] Focused unit, database, type, lint, formatting, architecture, migration, configuration,
      records, generated evidence, affected builds, and account browser gates pass without
      repeating the full workspace matrix.

## Risks and rollback

The main risks are bundled consent, old anonymous grants reviving after merge, cached grants
surviving withdrawal, client-selected notice authority, mutation of evidence, private text leaking
to analytics/AI metadata, or accidentally activating an unapproved external path. Rollback removes
the UI/API/gate and stops new writes while retaining append-only history. Production activation
and legal text remain owner gates.

## Verification evidence

- Focused domain/Web tests cover strict purpose/version parsing, private response shapes,
  same-origin/session-CSRF/idempotency enforcement, proxy allowlisting, and repeated AI
  selected-excerpt authorization checks.
- `pnpm test:account-consent-database` applies all 24 migrations and verifies defaults,
  cross-account isolation, cross-session grant/withdraw visibility, exact replay/conflict,
  concurrent contiguous sequences, stale-version denial, 257-record fail-closed behavior, and
  runtime update/delete denial.
- The affected account-control Chromium gate verifies three independent default-off controls,
  committed success, cross-session immediate withdrawal, mobile layout, keyboard flow, privacy,
  and accessibility.
- Production analytics, provider private-content AI, real-user model improvement, marketing,
  service-notification delivery, legal activation, deployment, and launch remain safe-off.
