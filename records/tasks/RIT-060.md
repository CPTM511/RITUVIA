# RIT-060: Versioned Country Policy Engine

- Backlog item: RIT-060
- Related records: D-059

The queue state, priority, milestone, dependencies, and owner gates remain canonical in
[BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).

## Outcome

Provide one strict, server-authoritative, immutable Country Policy Engine whose selected version
controls service availability, age requirements, modalities, products, fiat and crypto payment
capabilities, required disclosures and legal versions, data handling, locale/support, and
marketing constraints while recording the exact policy version on commercial records.

## Scope

Included: a dependency-free versioned policy contract; strict parsing and fail-closed evaluation;
effective windows, review expiry, disabled/rollback/kill-switch behavior, policy conflict
detection, declared/billing/geolocation country-evidence precedence, product/payment/recurring
decisions, required disclosure snapshots, immutable approval evidence, dry-run comparison, an
append-only database registry, least-privilege runtime reading, local synthetic policy fixtures,
focused unit/PostgreSQL/commerce tests, and canonical task/status records.

Excluded: legal conclusions from IP alone; production country activation; live Stripe or crypto;
pricing, taxes, refunds, catalog publication, or legal-policy approval; production policy
administration UI; destructive migration; deployment; public launch; and golden screenshot
updates.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Missing, malformed, overlapping, stale, future, disabled, unsupported, or unapproved policy
      data fails closed with a calm finite reason.
- [x] One immutable selected version controls service status, minimum age, modalities, products,
      subscriptions, fiat providers/methods/currencies, crypto, disclosures/legal versions, data
      flags, locale/support, and marketing restrictions.
- [x] Paid and crypto capabilities require explicit matching approval evidence; local synthetic
      evidence can never authorize staging or production.
- [x] Country evidence follows the documented hierarchy and conflicts cannot be bypassed with IP
      or locale alone.
- [x] The runtime database identity can read bounded active policy data but cannot insert, update,
      delete, truncate, perform DDL, own objects, or reach a privileged role.
- [x] Commercial authorization records the exact immutable policy version and snapshot required to
      explain the decision.
- [x] Focused package, commerce, PostgreSQL migration/constraint/privilege/restore, type, lint,
      formatting, architecture, migration, CI-contract, secret, record, and affected-build gates
      pass without repeating the complete workspace suite.

## Planned files and verification

Primary areas are `@rituvia/country-policy`, an expand-only policy-registry migration and Prisma
schema, a bounded database policy reader, local/CI database role provisioning, Web commerce policy
composition, focused tests, and canonical status/decision/evidence records.

Run strict parser/evaluator tests first, then focused commerce tests and the isolated PostgreSQL
gate. Close with affected formatting, linting, type checking, architecture, migration, CI-contract,
secret, record, generated-evidence, and package/build verification. The complete payment integrity
matrix remains reserved for RIT-069.

## Risks and rollback

Main risks are silently permitting unsupported service or payment behavior, using weak country
evidence to override billing/account facts, stale or overlapping active versions, local evidence
escaping into production, mutable approval history, overprivileged database access, and orders
that cannot explain their governing policy. Rollback disables the affected version or selects the
prior immutable approved version; production publication and every real provider/country/legal
activation remain owner gates.
