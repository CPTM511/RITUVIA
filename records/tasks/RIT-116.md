# RIT-116: Visible GEO answer, source, review, and entity authority

- Backlog item: RIT-116
- Related records: D-083, D-086
- Owner approval: Not required for the bounded reviewed English implementation and validation;
  another locale, route, tradition, structured-data claim, analytics provider, deployment, DNS,
  indexing activation, or public launch remains owner-gated

The queue state, priority, milestone, dependencies, and owner gates remain canonical in
[BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).

## Outcome

Give every approved English public page one visible, source-bound explanation of the entity being
discussed, the type of each claim, and the current review authority without inventing expertise or
adding crawler-only content.

## Scope

Included: one strict English GEO content record; five stable entity definitions; four closed claim
classifications; one shared server component; exact 45-route fail-closed resolution; approved
source-title projection; owner-review dates; public-page quality source/digest integration;
static, build-artifact, no-JavaScript, 320 px, accessibility, injection, privacy, and network QA.

Excluded: new pages; programmatic doorway expansion; user-specific answers; private prompts or
results; public author profiles; expert, religious, scientific, or institutional endorsements;
internal paths, IDs, hashes, locators, or reviewer identities; richer JSON-LD; FAQ/HowTo schema;
analytics; another locale or tradition; deployment, DNS, Search Console, actual indexing, and
public launch.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every exact approved public route keeps its direct answer near the top and renders exactly
      one visible semantic answer-authority section in server HTML.
- [x] Five stable entities provide one consistent name and definition across core, numerology,
      Western natal astrology, Major Arcana, and original-secular reflection pages.
- [x] The visible classification vocabulary distinguishes fact or method, tradition,
      interpretation, and product policy without presenting symbolic claims as facts.
- [x] Human-readable source titles and versions plus truthful owner-review dates replace
      inconsistent family-specific source/review fragments.
- [x] Internal source paths, checksums, locators, author IDs, reviewer IDs, fake expertise,
      private inputs, hidden copy, and unsafe markup cannot enter the projection.
- [x] Missing, duplicate, unknown, stale, unreviewed, or expired route/content authority fails
      closed.
- [x] The 45-page quality inventory includes the GEO source and visible content in deterministic
      source/content digests.
- [x] Existing minimal JSON-LD remains unchanged and receives no unsupported authority fields.
- [x] Focused static, content, build, no-JavaScript, mobile, Axe, injection, and network checks
      pass without rerunning the complete workspace test matrix.

## Risks and rollback

The compact authority section repeats family-level definitions across related pages. That
repetition is deliberate governance context and does not replace each page's distinct answer,
examples, tables, card meanings, spread instructions, or ritual steps. RIT-117 may measure
freshness and search performance but must not turn this contract into bot-only text or add
third-party tracking without approval.

Rollback removes the component and content record, restores the previous family source fragments,
regenerates the 45-page inventory, and removes the build/browser assertions. No migration,
persisted user data, or external state requires reversal.
