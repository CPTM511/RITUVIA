# RIT-167: Run the Owner core-loop acceptance walkthrough and record findings

- Backlog item: RIT-167
- Related records: D-095, D-097, D-098

The queue state, priority, milestone, dependencies, and owner gates remain canonical in
[BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).

## Outcome

Have the Owner follow the exact intake-first anonymous reflection journey in a protected review
environment and record either explicit acceptance or a prioritized, reproducible problem list that
can drive the next bounded task.

## Scope

Included: open the detailed Chinese manual beside the product; walk from homepage intake through
allowed/reframed/blocked/crisis behavior, one-card reading, intention, small action, free ritual,
private reflection, and Revisit; check desktop, 320px mobile, keyboard, and reduced-motion use;
record the exact page, button, expected result, actual result, severity, and evidence for every
finding; record the Owner acceptance decision.

Excluded: implementing fixes during the walkthrough, restoring frozen product expansion, public or
Beta deployment, production activation, payment, pricing, legal text, production AI, new locales or
countries, golden screenshot updates, or treating automated tests as the Owner's acceptance.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The Owner starts from `/en`, uses `Begin a free reading`, and completes the exact manual path
      through Revisit in the protected review environment without account or payment.
- [x] Allowed, reframed, blocked, crisis, offline/unavailable recovery, direct one-card validity,
      keyboard, 320px mobile, and reduced-motion behavior are reviewed against the manual.
- [x] Every discrepancy records route, visible control, reproduction steps, expected result, actual
      result, severity, evidence, and whether it blocks protected Beta acceptance.
- [x] The Owner records either explicit retained-journey acceptance or a prioritized problem list;
      no frozen expansion scope becomes Ready implicitly.
- [x] `BACKLOG.md`, `PROJECT_STATUS.md`, the Owner capability map, launch plan, and any resulting
      bounded follow-up task reflect the Owner's decision without claiming deployment or launch.

## Verification evidence

The protected local homepage and intake returned HTTP 200, and the product, exact Chinese button
manual, and launch plan were opened together in the Codex right panel. The Owner then replied on
2026-08-01: `接受。然后继续工作`. No prioritized discrepancy was reported.

RIT-166 production-build, full-loop, privacy, keyboard, mobile, reduced-motion, offline/unavailable,
configuration-boundary, and accessibility evidence remains the technical prerequisite. The Owner's
explicit response supplies the separate product acceptance required by D-095; it does not approve
Beta deployment, public launch, payment, production AI, or any other gated action.

## Risks and rollback

The principal risk is turning the walkthrough into unbounded redesign or treating a passing local
demo as release approval. Findings must stay reproducible and prioritized, and every production,
legal, payment, AI, locale, country, deployment, and launch gate remains separate.

Rollback removes only the Ready queue assignment if the review environment is unavailable or the
Owner defers the walkthrough. It does not reverse RIT-166, weaken intake, or restore frozen scope.
