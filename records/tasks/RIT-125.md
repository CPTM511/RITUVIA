## Outcome

Provide one privacy-minimal, source-bound operational case kernel that can triage, escalate, and
resolve support, privacy, safety, and content reports without granting operators access to private
source content or claiming an approved production support service.

## Scope

Included: additive support-ticket and operational-case schema; source foreign keys; transactional
enqueue from new reading reports, privacy exports, privacy deletions, and refund requests; local
policy-derived priority/SLA/draft metadata; role-specific step-up authorization; bounded list;
append-only transition and tamper-evident audit timelines; least-privilege grants; logical restore;
runbook, user-manual truth, decision and status records.

Excluded: historical source backfill; free-text support, attachments, or private evidence; ordinary
Support UI; admin HTTP/UI dashboard; external send/paging/status providers; production SLA,
retention, contact address, staffing, deployment, data mutation, or launch.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] All four queues share one source-bound case model with exactly one valid source foreign key.
- [x] New source writes enqueue transactionally; queue, priority, local due times, draft, and expiry
      are database-derived rather than caller-authored.
- [x] Cases, drafts, events, audits, logs, and tests contain no question, reading prose, journal,
      prayer, birth data, attachment, email, token, or arbitrary support message.
- [x] Queue listing and transitions require allowed role, recent authentication, same-session
      passkey MFA, reason, and ticket; wrong roles and raw source reads fail closed.
- [x] Triage, escalation, resolution, exact replay, changed-request conflict, SLA breach, stable
      ordering, append-only audit integrity, and logical restore pass in isolated PostgreSQL.
- [x] Draft automation selects one fixed English acknowledgement and never sends it.
- [x] Migration, schema-drift, rollback, retention, user-button, operator-runbook, and production
      approval boundaries are documented.

## Risks and rollback

The local due times are test policy, not a public promise. A queue without approved contact,
staffing, dashboard, external alerting, and standing staging does not complete Gate H. Historical
sources are intentionally not backfilled; any replay requires a separate bounded, reviewed plan.

Rollback disables enqueue and operator-service calls while retaining additive records and audit
history. Dropping tables, deleting rows, changing retention, sending drafts, or activating a
production support channel requires a later reviewed forward change and applicable Owner approval.
