# RIT-021: Safe question and theme intake

- Backlog item: RIT-021
- Related records: D-029, D-030

The queue state, priority, milestone, dependencies, and owner gates remain canonical in
[BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).

## Outcome

An anonymous user can choose one of ten English reflection themes and optionally submit a bounded
question for a deterministic, privacy-minimal `allowed`, `reframed`, `blocked`, or `crisis`
classification before any reading exists. The slice remains safe-off after the owner approved the
exact English safety language and activation policy through OWN-009, as recorded in D-030.

## Scope

Included: a framework-independent English question-intake policy and fixtures; strict normalized
input and public-output contracts; exact same-origin `POST /api/v1/intake/evaluate`; a private,
noindex, no-store `/en/intake` page; ten unselected native theme radios; an optional 600-character
question kept in page memory; explicit safer-question selection; loading, validation, offline,
unavailable, error, allowed, reframed, blocked, and crisis states; stale-response cancellation;
typed analytics projections without raw question text; a separate production activation reference;
and unit, build, HTTP-boundary, accessibility, and browser evidence.

Excluded: creating or storing a reading; persisting an intake, question, risk category, or
suggestion; emitting product analytics; AI/model/provider calls; probabilistic moderation;
country-specific crisis directories; professional advice; legal or clinical claims; another locale;
anonymous-session creation; production activation or deployment; and the stronger pre-generation
policy owned by RIT-032.

## Owner decision request: OWN-009

The owner approved this exact English safety-language set for the first intake policy on
2026-07-17; D-030 records its bounded effect:

- Boundary: “Symbolic reflection can offer perspective. It cannot predict outcomes or replace
  professional support.”
- Reframed title: “A gentler question keeps the choice with you”
- Reframed message: “This wording asks for certainty or falls outside the reviewed
  agency-preserving patterns. You can explicitly choose the safer question below and review it
  again.”
- Blocked title: “This question is outside symbolic reflection”
- Blocked message: “RITUVIA cannot determine or interpret this high-stakes or harmful request. You
  can choose the safer question below and review it again.”
- Crisis title: “Pause this reflection and get immediate support”
- Crisis message: “If you or someone else may be in immediate danger or at risk of self-harm,
  contact local emergency services now. Reach a trusted person who can stay with the person at
  risk. RITUVIA cannot provide emergency help.”
- Professional-preparation suggestion: “What questions could I prepare for a qualified
  professional?”
- Relationship-agency suggestion: “What boundaries or actions are within my control in this
  relationship?”
- Grounding suggestion: “What observable facts and supportive steps could help me feel grounded?”

Approved scope: English only; deterministic lexical baseline; no country-specific hotline or
assumption that one emergency number is global; generic local emergency-services and trusted-person
direction; no continuation after crisis; no persistent intake data or analytics emission; and no
production deployment, public-shell activation, or regional/language launch granted by this
approval. The production eligibility reference is `own-009.question-intake.en.v1`; this does not
activate or deploy the feature.

## Risks and rollback

The deterministic policy is deliberately conservative: unknown free text is reframed, so it can
over-block harmless phrasing and cannot detect every obfuscation or nuanced high-stakes request.
RIT-032 must add the reviewed pre-generation safety layer before any divination service consumes
intake. Generic emergency direction avoids publishing the wrong regional number, but it is not a
country-specific crisis-resource program and requires the owner decision above. No raw question is
stored, so the policy cannot retrospectively audit individual text; only fixed, text-free outcome
projections are available for future approved analytics.

Rollback by leaving `RITUVIA_QUESTION_INTAKE_ACTIVATION_REFERENCE` empty or disabling the existing
public-shell flag, then reverting the private route, API, form, message, and domain-policy code.
There is no migration or retained intake data to reverse or delete. Production deployment and any
future safety-policy weakening remain separate owner gates.
