## Risks and rollback

Local checks cannot establish cultural quality, legal sufficiency, search ranking, or production
crawl behavior. English-only content must not be machine-published in another locale. Any copy
change after approval requires a new checksum and review.

Rollback removes the five approved routes from public discovery, restores `noindex`, and can remove
the pages, metadata, styles, tests, and browser verifier. The private RIT-094 natal facts viewer,
D-070 calculation method, RIT-093 engine, and RIT-095 safe-off interpretation boundary remain
unchanged. There is no migration, customer data, production state, secret, payment, deployment, or
remote action to reverse.
