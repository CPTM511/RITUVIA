# RIT-032: Pre-generation high-stakes and crisis policy

- Backlog item: RIT-032
- Related records: D-030, D-033, D-037, D-038, D-039

The queue state, priority, milestone, dependencies, and owner gates remain canonical in
[BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).

## Outcome

The AI package now re-evaluates every bounded intake request, stops every non-allowed route before
downstream work, and issues an opaque request-bound generation authorization only after exact
classifier and safety-policy authority checks succeed.

## Scope

Included: pure English/Tarot safety contracts; canonical RIT-021 re-evaluation; injected exact
classifier registration and authority; strict classifier output; conservative route precedence;
shared intake rejection of malformed lone-surrogate text; transient question and risk-category
handling; fixed suggestion codes; allowed-only continuation; separate policy-activation authority;
request-bound opaque authorization; adversarial synthetic fixtures; compiled-build and architecture
evidence.

Excluded: provider/model selection or invocation, credentials, production classifier registration
or policy activation, crisis copy/resources, API/UI/runtime composition, divination execution,
retrieval/prompt assembly, generated-prose verification, persistence, analytics, other locales or
modalities, payments, and deployment.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The gate accepts bounded raw JSON and independently re-runs the pinned
      `question-intake.en.v1` parser/evaluator instead of trusting client-created safety values.
- [x] Lexical crisis stops immediately; every other non-empty question requires an exact,
      externally authorized classifier, while theme-only input exposes no question and needs no
      classifier.
- [x] `crisis > blocked > reframed > allowed` precedence prevents downgrades; uncertainty stays at
      least reframed, and crisis returns no suggestion.
- [x] Missing, denied, throwing, malformed, oversized, duplicate, out-of-order, or registration-
      mismatched classifier evidence fails closed without invoking downstream continuation.
- [x] Raw question and classifier categories are transient and absent from evaluation, decision,
      authorization, errors, serialized results, persistence, logs, and analytics contracts.
- [x] Only an allowed evaluation with a separately authorized policy approval can mint an opaque
      authorization bound to request, modality, reading type, locale, theme, intake policy, safety
      policy, and approval reference; issuers are not root exports.
- [x] Reviewed synthetic fixtures prove every unsafe/invalid case has zero continuation calls and
      allowed cases continue exactly once without a provider, network, database, or credential.
- [x] Focused unit, type, lint, architecture, compiled-workspace, configuration-boundary, and
      repository evidence gates pass without activating an owner-gated runtime.

## Verification evidence

- Synthetic fixtures exercise all routes, mixed-category precedence, uncertainty, no-downgrade,
  nuanced categories, safe-context false positives, and private canaries.
- Tests inject only synthetic classifier and authority callbacks and assert exact invocation counts,
  failure codes, frozen output, forged-value rejection, and request-binding mismatch.
- The production artifact verifier requires the safety JavaScript/declaration artifacts, proves
  crisis cannot continue, proves allowed request binding, and rejects public issuer exports.
- Full repository quality-gate results are recorded in `PROJECT_STATUS.md` and the final task result
  after generated evidence is synchronized.

## Risks and rollback

The future Web/server composition must supply exact owner-approved classifier and policy mappings,
keep question text out of logs and persistence, and validate the full authorization binding before
provider use. RIT-034 still owns post-generation safety/fact verification, and RIT-036 owns the
release eval suite. No production policy, classifier, model, provider, or crisis flow is approved by
this task.

Rollback removes the unused safety module, authorization-binding extension, intake parser
lone-surrogate rejection, fixtures, tests, and build evidence. There is no migration, stored data,
provider side effect, secret, or destructive cleanup.
