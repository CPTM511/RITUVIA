features at provider discretion, provides no correctness or fitness guarantee, and has no private
support or availability/response SLA. RITUVIA owns validation, availability, incident response,
MTTR, native maintenance, and replacement capacity. AGPL removes the purchase requirement but adds
exact deployed Corresponding Source and whole-project compatibility obligations.

Rollback removes the safe-off selection catalog, parser, exports, tests, and records. No
third-party code or data, birth data, migration, provider request, payment, contract execution,
secret, deployment, or public behavior exists to reverse.
