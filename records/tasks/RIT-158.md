## Outcome

RIT-158 consolidates the already implemented foundations into one local, responsive, English
commercial MVP candidate shaped by the Lumora interactive prototype and business plan without
weakening RITUVIA's safety, privacy, payment, or approval boundaries.

## Scope

Included: anonymous deterministic reading; account sign-in and sign-out; exact persisted
`age-18.local.v1` attestation before paid order creation; intention plus one small real-world
action; free candle/incense and entitlement-gated owned paid ritual objects; encrypted private
journal entries with a revisit time; server-authoritative product catalog and integer prices;
hosted-checkout abstraction with a Stripe adapter; an HMAC-signed local checkout simulator;
raw-body signed webhook verification; idempotent order, append-only ledger, and entitlement
fulfillment; and responsive English account, checkout, sanctuary, error, retry, and degraded states.

Excluded: live Stripe keys or checkout sessions, provider onboarding or written underwriting,
real charges, production legal/tax/country activation, public deployment or launch, production AI
provider/content activation, subscription operations, and any claim that payment improves ritual
efficacy.

- [x] The two Lumora HTML artifacts guide local product shape, while `AGENTS.md` safety, privacy,

- [x] Root's final production-artifact Playwright flow passes the anonymous reading, account,

## Risks and rollback

The local authentication and signed checkout simulator are development capabilities, not evidence
of a production identity or payment-provider relationship. The exact 18+ control is an attestation,
not country-specific age verification. Production still requires underwriting, legal/tax/country
policy, credentials, operational controls, and owner go/no-go approval.
Production activation also requires managed-database role capability evidence and a dedicated
owner-approved reflection privacy/retention activation reference; neither blocks this local MVP.

Rollback disables the local-MVP configuration and reverts the RIT-158 application slice. Its
expand-only local schema can remain inert; a disposable local database may be rebuilt rather than
using a destructive shared-data rollback. No production data, provider account, charge, DNS,
deployment, or customer state is changed by this local candidate.
