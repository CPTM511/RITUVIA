# RIT-061: Immutable Catalog, Product, and Price Registry

- Backlog item: RIT-061
- Related records: D-060

The queue state, priority, milestone, dependencies, and owner gates remain canonical in
[BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md).

## Outcome

Provide one server-authoritative, immutable, locale-aware catalog whose exact products, digital
contents, Credit costs, fiat prices, country/provider eligibility, tax category, refund policy,
effective window, and source evidence can be validated, seeded, read with least privilege, and
snapshotted by later commercial records.

## Scope

Included: a strict dependency-free catalog contract; product kinds for Credit packs, Plus plans,
Deep Readings, permanent objects, consumable rituals, and free objects; reviewed English and
Simplified Chinese localizations; exact digital contents; integer USD prices only for fiat-priced
products; Credit cost/grant/cadence fields; immutable version/source evidence; a synthetic local/CI
seed derived from the owner-approved production contract; an append-only PostgreSQL registry;
bounded runtime reading; focused domain, Web adapter, migration, constraint, privilege, seed,
restore, and build evidence; and canonical status records.

Excluded: production catalog publication or pricing activation; pricing, tax, refund, legal, or
country approval; live Stripe or crypto; orders, payment attempts, Credit ledger/reservations,
entitlement grants, subscription lifecycle, checkout UX, admin UI, destructive migration,
deployment, public launch, and golden screenshot updates.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Catalog, product, localization, and price records use strict immutable versions and reject
      unknown fields, invalid combinations, duplicate identities, overlapping active prices, and
      non-integer or non-positive money.
- [x] The catalog contains the exact owner-approved product identifiers, Credit costs/grants,
      billing cadences, provider eligibility, and reviewed English/Simplified Chinese contents
      without treating Credits as money or transferable value.
- [x] Fiat prices carry currency, countries, provider eligibility, tax category, refund policy,
      and effective windows; Credit-priced and free products have no fiat amount.
- [x] Only a synthetic local/CI catalog can be seeded; preview, staging, and production remain
      empty and safe-off pending explicit owner approvals.
- [x] Runtime can read one bounded active catalog but cannot insert, update, delete, truncate,
      perform DDL, own objects, or reach the control role.
- [x] Focused package, PostgreSQL, type, lint, formatting, architecture, migration, CI-contract,
      secret, record, generated-evidence, and affected-build gates pass without repeating the
      complete workspace suite.

## Risks and rollback

Main risks are silently changing exact product contents, representing permanent or consumable
Credit products as direct money purchases, allowing mutable price history, publishing local
evidence outside local/CI, overprivileged catalog access, or activating unapproved prices/providers.
Rollback selects no active catalog or appends a reviewed successor; production publication and all
real pricing/provider/legal/tax/refund decisions remain owner gates.
