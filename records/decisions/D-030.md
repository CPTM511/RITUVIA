# D-030: English question-intake safety language and activation eligibility

- Date: 2026-07-17
- Owners: owner, ai_safety, product, privacy, qa_security
- Related tasks: OWN-009, RIT-021, RIT-024, RIT-032
- Evidence references: records/tasks/RIT-021.md, packages/domain/src/question-intake.ts, apps/web/app/_i18n/question-intake-messages.ts, packages/config/src/server.ts

## Context

RIT-021 needs exact English language for a deterministic pre-reading intake that can allow,
reframe, block, or stop a question. D-025 explicitly limited the existing public safety page to
product boundaries rather than an activated crisis flow, so crisis wording, generic emergency
direction, and activation eligibility require a separate owner decision. The product is global,
but no country-specific crisis-resource registry, reviewed hotline directory, or additional locale
exists. The implementation also must not imply diagnosis, emergency service, prediction, or a
continuation into a reading that has not yet been built.

## Decision

Approve the exact English boundary, reframed, blocked, crisis, and safer-question language recorded
under `Owner decision request: OWN-009` in `records/tasks/RIT-021.md`, with policy version
`question-intake.en.v1`. The crisis message directs a person to local emergency services and a
trusted person without publishing a universal number or country-specific resource. A crisis result
clears the question and cannot continue. Blocked and reframed results may offer only one fixed,
explicitly user-selected agency-preserving question; the product never silently rewrites input.

Approve production configuration eligibility only through the exact reference
`own-009.question-intake.en.v1`. Missing configuration remains safe-off, and other `own-*`, `test.*`,
or malformed references remain invalid in production. This decision does not activate a feature
flag, deploy the product, launch another language/country, approve a crisis-resource directory, or
weaken any later RIT-032 pre-generation safety gate.

Keep the intake transient: raw question text and internal risk categories are excluded from URLs,
metadata, persistence, logs, responses, and analytics. No analytics emitter is approved. The
deterministic lexical policy remains an intentionally conservative first boundary, not a medical,
legal, financial, emergency, or probabilistic moderation service.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Benefits | Costs/risks | Why not selected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Publish one global hotline number | Short and specific | Incorrect or inaccessible across countries; implies an approved regional resource program | Generic local emergency direction is more truthful for the current scope |
| Allow all unknown free text | Lower friction | Unsafe false negatives and unreviewed high-stakes continuation | Unknown free text is reframed conservatively |
| Automatically replace unsafe wording | Faster continuation | Removes user agency and can hide the original safety boundary | Suggestions require an explicit user action and a new review |
| Store raw questions for audit | Easier retrospective inspection | Creates sensitive retention, access, breach, and deletion obligations before necessity review | Transient processing is the minimum useful data path |
| Treat this approval as production activation | Fewer gates | Conflates reviewed copy with deploy, country, domain, and operational readiness | Eligibility and activation remain separate auditable decisions |

## Consequences

RIT-021 can complete with an exact, owner-approved English safety surface and a fail-closed
production reference. Users in crisis receive direct generic action rather than divination or a
suggestion. The generic message is less locally useful than an approved country resource directory,
and the lexical policy can over-reframe harmless wording or miss nuanced unsafe language. RIT-032
must provide the stronger pre-generation policy before a reading service consumes intake.

No private content, new database object, migration, analytics event emission, country, locale, or
production operation is approved. Changing any approved safety sentence, crisis routing behavior,
or production policy reference requires a superseding reviewed decision.

## Validation

Domain fixtures must cover all four states, ordering `crisis > blocked > reframed > allowed`, common
obfuscation, false-positive controls, strict canonical output, and privacy-safe analytics
projection. Web and HTTP tests must cover exact same-origin routing, safe-off configuration,
noindex/no-store responses, no-JavaScript, explicit suggestion adoption, crisis stop/clear behavior,
offline/error states, stale-response rejection, and raw-text canaries. Production builds,
architecture, accessibility, and browser checks must pass before RIT-021 is Done.

Reconsider when another locale/country is proposed, a reviewed crisis-resource registry exists,
RIT-032 introduces a stronger policy, evidence shows material false-positive/negative harm, or any
approved wording changes.

## Rollout and rollback

Keep `RITUVIA_QUESTION_INTAKE_ACTIVATION_REFERENCE` empty until a separately approved deployment
sets the exact reference. Roll back immediately by removing that configuration or disabling the
existing public-shell flag. The route/API then return a private non-cacheable 404. No database or
retained intake data requires migration or deletion.
