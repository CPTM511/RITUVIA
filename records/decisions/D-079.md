# D-079: Development-stage launch, age, refund, and growth baseline

- Date: 2026-07-28
- Owners: owner, product, legal, payments_risk, growth_seo, localization
- Related tasks: OWN-004, RIT-105
- Evidence references: owner approval in the 2026-07-28 Codex task;
  `docs/08_I18N_SEO_GEO_GROWTH.md`; `ROADMAP.md`

## Context

RITUVIA needs stable development assumptions for account eligibility, draft policy architecture,
country and locale sequencing, refund behavior, and SEO/GEO/ASO work. The repository is still in
development, testing, and deployment preparation. Exact registered-company particulars, qualified
legal review, tax or Merchant of Record selection, production payment approval, final policy text,
and public launch authorization do not yet exist.

Treating a practical development direction as final legal approval would incorrectly close
`OWN-004` and could activate country, payment, privacy, or refund behavior without the evidence
required by the human approval gates.

## Decision

Use IPO.ONE's BVI company as the intended contracting-entity direction while keeping its exact
registered legal name, company number, registered address, and authorized signatory unresolved
until documentary evidence is supplied.

Design the complete platform, account, and paid-feature experience for adults aged 18 or older.
This is a product-policy baseline, not a representation that age assurance, local minority rules,
or every country's eligibility review is complete.

Use the United States and English as the first production-launch candidate. Permit `es-419`,
`pt-BR`, and `fr` only in closed, non-indexed testing for United States participants after their
content review gates pass. Treat Mexico and Brazil as later country candidates. Defer France and
other European Union launches until consumer, privacy, tax, support, withdrawal, and payment
requirements receive qualified review. No country or non-English locale is activated by this
decision.

Draft refund behavior around no refund after access to a supplied digital experience, while always
preserving mandatory-law remedies, duplicate or unauthorized charge review, non-delivery, and a
material product defect that is not cured within the documented support process. Subscription
cancellation stops future renewals. Exact legal language, support evidence standards, timelines,
dispute handling, automated-refund thresholds, and provider configuration remain unresolved.

Continue technical SEO and GEO during development using useful, source-transparent, human-first,
server-rendered content. Prepare ASO research, naming, metadata, screenshot plans, and keyword
evidence only; do not create an app-store release or claim native-app readiness unless later PWA
behavior and economics justify a native product.

Allow local, test, and protected preview/staging work. Keep production deployment, DNS, unrestricted
public indexing, payment activation, production messaging, final legal-policy activation, and
app-store submission behind their existing explicit owner gates.

`OWN-004` remains `Blocked` until the exact entity particulars, launch-country legal analysis,
qualified reviewer or counsel, final terms/privacy/refund language, and tax or Merchant of Record
path are documented.

## Alternatives considered

| Option                                                      | Benefits                                        | Costs/risks                                                | Decision |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Treat the approval as final legal launch authority          | Fastest apparent path                           | Invents missing entity, counsel, tax, and country evidence | Rejected |
| Block all product and growth development                    | Avoids provisional assumptions                  | Prevents safe independent engineering and content work     | Rejected |
| Record a development baseline and preserve activation gates | Stable architecture without false legal closure | Requires later evidence and policy replacement             | Selected |

## Consequences

Product and content code can consistently model adult access, phased locale/country rollout,
future-cancellation behavior, and non-indexed localization testing. Draft policy documents must
identify themselves as drafts and cannot be used as active consent or checkout terms.

Country policy, payments, support, tax, legal copy, public indexing, email, deployment, and native
distribution remain safe-off. Later qualified advice or provider requirements may supersede this
baseline through a new decision without rewriting this record.

## Validation

Backlog and project-status checks must continue to report `OWN-004` and `RIT-105` as blocked.
Country and locale tests must prove that only English and the United States can become launch
candidates under a separately approved policy version, while closed-test locales remain
non-indexed and non-production. Refund, payment, legal, deployment, and app-store changes require
their task-specific tests and approval evidence before activation.

## Rollout and rollback

This record changes development assumptions and documentation only. It does not mutate data,
publish legal terms, activate a locale or country, enable a provider, deploy, change DNS, send
messages, or submit an application. Roll back by superseding this decision and updating dependent
drafts and policy fixtures before any production activation.
