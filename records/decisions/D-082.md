## Consequences

The approved artifact enters the shared editorial registry and the six routes enter the localized
public route registry, canonical metadata, production-only robots allowlist, and English ritual
sitemap. The private Sanctuary and private reflection data remain private and noindex. Search
indexing still cannot occur in local, preview, or staging environments, or while the production
public shell is disabled.

Future body-copy, route, rights, source, review, locale, or publication-scope changes require a new
reviewed artifact and authority update. Future translations require their own exact source binding,
human review, locale activation, and support readiness.

## Validation

Focused contract tests must pin the reviewed candidate digest, approved artifact digest, source
catalog digest, exact six-route inventory, shared editorial authority, metadata polarity, and
robots/sitemap containment. Production-build and browser checks must cover the hub, one virtual
ritual guide, and one private-reflection guide for canonical metadata, structured data, responsive
layout, accessibility, console/network errors, and private-data absence.

## Rollout and rollback

No database migration, AI provider, payment, country, locale, deployment, DNS, or public-launch
action is authorized. Roll back by removing the ritual/reflection definitions from the public
registry and shared editorial registry, returning metadata to noindex, and retaining the source and
decision records for audit.
