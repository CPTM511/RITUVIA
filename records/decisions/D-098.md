## Context

The Owner accepted the 2026-08-03 recovery audit's NO-GO conclusion, selected
`f79fee6713670fdc12b33dd3182569a942782636` as the recovery baseline, preserved the original
243-file staged snapshot without merging it, kept AGPL-3.0-only, and authorized a bounded
Founder Acceptance Recovery in protected staging. The recovery authority was intentionally split
from every production, DNS, public-release, real-funds, Provider-activation, and legal gate.

Item 11 later proved bounded Provider AI journeys FJ-16/FJ-17 but could not reach a Coinbase hosted
checkout because the account lacked Coinbase Business Checkout entitlement. On 2026-08-10 the
Owner directed Codex not to execute FJ-15 in this recovery, preserved non-custodial crypto payment
as a future option, and explicitly approved Item 12.

## Decision

Use the twelve-item Recovery Backlog as the only recovery queue, one explicitly approved item per
Codex run. Keep the recovery branch descended from the approved baseline and exclude the archival
snapshot commit. Use one protected, authenticated, noindex, isolated staging environment for Items
3-12 and stop after every item.

For the current Item 12 gate:

- mandatory journeys are FJ-00-FJ-14 and FJ-16-FJ-20;
- FJ-15 is the sole explicit Owner-approved exclusion and must not be executed;
- the previous Coinbase `403` / application `503` remains fail-closed evidence, not acceptance;
- existing Coinbase Sandbox integration remains safe-off and may be reused only after a new,
  separately scoped Owner approval; and
- future crypto payment remains possible only as third-party-hosted, non-custodial checkout after
  Provider, legal, security, sandbox, production, and real-value gates are separately satisfied.

## Consequences

Item 11 is complete for the amended recovery scope on its accepted FJ-16/FJ-17 evidence. No claim
is made that Coinbase hosted checkout passed. Item 12 may run the final protected-staging Founder
Acceptance, security, restore, rollback, and source-disclosure gate without FJ-15.

Even if Item 12 passes, the repository remains NO-GO for production until a later Owner decision
nominates a production candidate and separately resolves every retained production gate. A future
crypto item must begin with a new before-state, objective acceptance test, Provider entitlement,
legal/security scope, and explicit exclusion of real value unless separately authorized.

## Rollout and rollback

This amendment changes governance and acceptance scope only. It does not require deleting Coinbase
code, webhooks, records, migrations, or evidence. Rollback means recording a later Owner decision
that restores FJ-15 to a newly approved future item; it never means silently executing crypto
checkout, weakening safe-off controls, or rewriting history.
