## Context

The production-pack contract defines Credit packs, Plus plans, Deep Readings, permanent objects,
free objects, consumable rituals, exact Credit costs/grants, USD prototype prices, and provider
compatibility. It does not contain all locale copy, exact digital contents, country/tax/refund
fields, effective windows, or publication evidence required by RIT-061. The reviewed golden
prototype supplies English/Simplified Chinese product copy and exact Deep Reading inputs/outputs.

The existing local commerce fixture separately offered four ritual objects for direct
USD 0.99–5.99 purchases, including the legacy `golden_intention_bowl` code. That model conflicts
with the production contract, where permanent objects consume Credits and the canonical code is
`golden_bowl`.

## Decision

Use one strict `catalog-version.v1` contract with an immutable catalog version, finite product
union, complete reviewed locales, exact digital contents, Credit terms, and separate fiat prices.
Only Credit packs and Plus plans may have fiat prices. Free, Deep Reading, permanent-object, and
consumable-ritual products carry no fiat amount. Plus annual allocates eight Credits monthly rather
than granting 96 upfront. Coinbase USDC on Base is compatible only with Credit packs; Plus remains
Stripe-only.

Persist normalized immutable catalog, product, localization, and price rows. Require positive
integer minor units, exact product-version foreign keys, effective windows, country/provider
eligibility, tax category, refund-policy version, source checksum/reference, and owner evidence.
The runtime receives bounded SELECT-only access with live privilege attestation. Control can append
but never mutate history.

Seed only the synthetic local/CI catalog derived from the production-pack contract and reviewed
prototype. Its `local_test` evidence and `test:` refund/tax references cannot authorize preview,
staging, or production. Country Policy remains the independent authorization source and the local
policy/provider intersection does not activate Stripe or Coinbase.

Remove the legacy direct-object list from the public `/api/v1/catalog` response. The old signed
local checkout remains only as a local compatibility/test fixture for historical order replay and
is not a RIT-061 product or production catalog source. Until RIT-066 implements Credit-aware
catalog UX, Sanctuary rejects the new schema and uses its existing free candle/incense degraded
path.

## Alternatives considered

| Option                                    | Benefits                  | Costs/risks                                         | Why not selected                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Keep four direct USD ritual objects       | Existing UI keeps working | Contradicts authoritative Credit model and SKU      | Product truth has priority                |
| Add fiat prices to every product          | Simple checkout           | Treats Credit content as direct money purchase      | Violates exact catalog contract           |
| Seed production disabled rows             | Explicit shape            | Can be mistaken for approved pricing/legal scope    | Production must remain empty              |
| Put all localized content in one JSON row | Fewer tables              | Weak locale uniqueness and auditability             | Exact localized versions need constraints |
| Delete legacy order code now              | Removes obsolete path     | Breaks historical replay before RIT-062 replacement | Quarantine is safer than deletion         |

## Consequences

The public catalog now fails closed when an environment has no exactly one current approved
version. Local/CI can inspect the complete source-derived catalog; preview/staging/production remain
empty. Existing Sanctuary paid cards disappear into the tested free fallback rather than showing
incorrect direct prices. RIT-062 can snapshot exact catalog/product/price versions, and RIT-066 can
render Credit-aware product details without changing catalog truth.

## Rollout and rollback

The migration is additive. Rollback serves no active catalog or appends a disabled/retired
successor; never edit or delete catalog history. The legacy local fixture can be removed only after
RIT-062/RIT-066 replace its tests and replay mapping. Production prices, tax/refund rules,
countries, providers, credentials, deployment, and public launch remain explicit owner gates.

## Validation

Focused tests cover strict unknown-field rejection, exact production-contract parity, Credit term
unions, locale completeness, integer money, provider/cadence constraints, overlapping country
scopes, exact seed counts, double-seed idempotency, RLS/privilege denial, logical dump/restore,
public API fail-closed behavior, and the Sanctuary free fallback contract.

## Sources and approvals

This decision implements the owner-approved production source pack for local development evidence.
It does not approve or activate production pricing, legal/tax/refund text, a country, Stripe,
Coinbase, crypto, subscription billing, deployment, or public launch.
