# D-083: Source-bound public-page quality authorization

- Date: 2026-07-29
- Owners: growth_seo, product, content, frontend, qa_security
- Related tasks: RIT-113, RIT-114, RIT-116, RIT-117
- Evidence references: `content/editorial/public-page-inventory.v1.json`;
  `packages/content/src/public-page-quality.ts`;
  `scripts/verify-public-page-quality.ts`;
  `apps/web/app/_i18n/public-page-inventory.ts`

## Context

The approval-bound localized route registry controls exact paths, locale, canonical identity,
robots, and sitemap membership, while the shared editorial registry controls source, rights,
review, and publication authority for four content families. Neither contract alone proves that
every active page remains substantive, unique, non-cannibalizing, internally connected, public,
non-personalized, and bound to its current rendered source. Independent validation scripts also
repeated the growing route list and could drift from the runtime registry.

## Decision

Create one deterministic 45-route quality inventory from the D-076 route registry, D-025 typed core
copy, D-080 editorial records, exact source files, and the reviewed internal-link graph. Every
record binds stable route and locale identity, content family and shape, intent ownership,
canonical path, authority reference, reviewed source-set digest, review freshness, content digest,
word and unique-word counts, substantive blocks, headings, internal links, and nearest-page
similarity evidence.

Use normalized Unicode word evidence, exact normalized-content digests, three-token Jaccard
similarity, five-token bidirectional containment, and family-shape minimums. Exact duplication,
near duplication at Jaccard `0.82` or containment `0.90`, and same-family/same-shape template
substitution at Jaccard `0.70` or containment `0.84` fail. These conservative thresholds preserve
the legitimate shared Tarot template while detecting title, keyword, or slug substitution and
short-page containment.

Treat publication as one atomic authorization. The checked-in compact inventory must match a fresh
recomputation from current sources and must cover the exact route registry with no missing or extra
record. Runtime canonical/hreflang, robots, and sitemap output require the complete inventory to be
current and passing. Missing, stale, malformed, duplicated, expired, private, personalized,
query-bearing, framework-representation, weak-quality, or unknown evidence suppresses canonical
alternates, emits noindex metadata, disallows all crawling, and emits no sitemap.

Keep D-025 as the source authority for the four existing typed core pages and bind their exact Web
message source digest. The other 41 routes require their existing shared editorial record and exact
source-set digest. This decision adds no public page, locale, country, content tradition, provider,
deployment, DNS change, index activation, or public launch.

## Alternatives considered

| Option                               | Benefits                         | Costs/risks                                                              | Decision |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------- |
| Trust route approval only            | Small runtime                    | Thin, copied, or stale content can still index                           | Rejected |
| Use external AI similarity review    | Flexible semantics               | Sends content externally and is nondeterministic                         | Rejected |
| Use one word-count threshold         | Simple                           | Misses padding, containment, template substitution, and family structure | Rejected |
| Deterministic source-bound inventory | Reviewable, offline, fail-closed | Requires regenerated evidence after reviewed copy changes                | Selected |

## Consequences

SEO publication now depends on current quality evidence as well as environment and public-shell
activation. A single invalid record suppresses the whole crawl inventory rather than publishing a
partially trusted set. Reviewed source changes require regenerating and reviewing the compact
inventory. Core pages remain decision-authorized rather than being silently represented as D-080
editorial records.

The gate detects deterministic structural and textual risks; it does not replace human cultural,
legal, editorial, or search-strategy review. Structured-data eligibility remains RIT-114, GEO
answer/entity templates remain RIT-116, and ongoing search/freshness operations remain RIT-117.

## Validation

Focused tests cover the exact 45 routes, stable source and intent evidence, missing coverage,
canonical/path drift, private routes, stale reviews, weak similarity evidence, exact duplicates,
near duplicates, short-page containment, template-only substitution, thin content, same-intent
cannibalization, unapproved/private/personalized exposure, and internal-link failure. CI and
repository evidence require exact source recomputation before build. The configuration boundary and
Web build policy consume the same inventory instead of maintaining another route list.

## Rollout and rollback

No migration or external state exists. Roll back by removing the quality inventory and restoring
D-076 as the crawl source, while retaining D-080 editorial publication authorization. Such a
rollback weakens the publication gate and requires a new reviewed decision before production use.
