# D-044: Lumora-reference local commercial MVP consolidation

- Date: 2026-07-18
- Owners: owner, product, frontend, backend, payments_risk, qa_security
- Related tasks: RIT-158
- Evidence references: reference/lumora_interactive_prototype.html, reference/lumora_business_plan_zh.html, apps/web/app/[locale]/sanctuary/page.tsx, packages/payments/src/adapters/stripe-hosted-checkout.ts, records/tasks/RIT-158.md

## Context

The repository had strong safety and engineering foundations but was advancing through isolated
milestone slices. The owner asked to prioritize a coherent, locally runnable commercial MVP shaped
by the supplied Lumora prototype and business plan so the full user and revenue loop could be
validated before finer iteration.

The prototype is product and visual evidence, not authority to weaken safety, privacy, payment,
cultural-integrity, or production-approval controls. Live payment onboarding, legal text, country
activation, production deployment, and public launch remain owner gates.

## Decision

Create one consolidated RIT-158 vertical slice rather than adding a new task for each small MVP
module. Use the two Lumora artifacts as the primary local reference for scope, interaction, and
visual direction. Preserve the higher-priority repository constraints for private data, truthful
reflection language, a meaningful free path, exact 18+ paid attestation, server-authoritative
prices, hosted checkout, signed idempotent webhooks, ledger reconciliation, and entitlements.

The local candidate covers anonymous reading, account sign-in/sign-out, intention and small action,
free and owned paid rituals, encrypted journal/revisit, a Stripe hosted-checkout adapter, and a
signed local checkout simulator. Local fulfillment must exercise the same order, payment-event,
ledger, and entitlement boundaries as a hosted provider, but it cannot charge money and is not
evidence of provider approval.

After owner testing, correct two acceptance regressions without creating a second MVP task.
Sanctuary must allow a standalone intention when no valid reading is present, as the canonical PRD
already requires. New local Tarot draws must use the owner-provided prototype's complete 22-card
Major Arcana identities and Rider-Waite-Smith ordering (Strength VIII, Justice XI), with bounded
RITUVIA-original English reflection copy. Preserve the superseded Threshold/Mirror/Lantern catalog
only in the exact-version registry so previously persisted readings remain replayable.

## Alternatives considered

| Option                                   | Benefits                | Costs/risks                                                                         | Why not selected                                               |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Continue only the prior milestone order  | Lowest process change   | Delays owner validation of the complete product and revenue loop                    | Consolidate one bounded local slice while retaining hard gates |
| Copy the static prototype as the product | Fast visual demo        | No durable identity, private data, payment integrity, accessibility, or operability | Implement the interaction in the production architecture       |
| Activate Stripe and deploy publicly now  | Immediate real charging | Missing provider, legal, country, credential, and release approvals                 | Keep adapters ready and production activation closed           |

## Consequences

The owner can validate a complete local MVP instead of disconnected screens. The repository gains
account, reflection, commerce, and private-data modules plus focused tests and browser evidence.
The broader roadmap is not marked complete: future production work still owns legal terms,
provider onboarding, country/tax policy, production secrets, monitoring, deployment, and go/no-go.

## Validation

RIT-158 requires repository quality gates, isolated PostgreSQL migration/privilege/reset/restore
evidence, and a real-browser flow from anonymous reading through account, 18+ checkout,
entitlement, owned ritual, private journal/revisit, and sign-out. The original production artifact
passed all 11 Playwright steps with zero serious/critical Axe findings, unexpected browser/network
errors, or responsive overflow. The corrective production-artifact pass also completed a direct
standalone Sanctuary intention, free ritual, and private journal and revealed a card from the
active 22-card Major Arcana, with zero serious/critical Axe, page, unexpected console, or
unexpected HTTP failures. RIT-158 is `Done`; production activation remains separately gated.

## Rollout and rollback

Rollout is local-only behind validated configuration and the existing safe-off activation plane.
Rollback disables that configuration and reverts the application slice. Expand-only local schema
may remain inert; disposable local data can be rebuilt. No production charge, customer data, DNS,
provider account, deployment, or public traffic is changed by this decision.

## Sources and approvals

Sources: owner direction in the active Codex task; `AGENTS.md`; the two versioned Lumora reference
