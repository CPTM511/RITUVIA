# D-088: Fail-closed offline AI operations metrics and review thresholds

- Date: 2026-07-29
- Owners: operations, ai_safety, privacy, qa_security, product
- Related tasks: RIT-038, RIT-120, RIT-127
- Evidence references: `packages/analytics/src/ai-operations.ts`;
  `scripts/generate-ai-operations-report.ts`; `scripts/verify-ai-operations.ts`;
  `packages/analytics/test/ai-operations.test.ts`; `tests/ai-operations-report.test.ts`

## Context

D-040 already authorizes bounded categorical generation metadata containing result, failure,
attempt, latency, validated nullable token/cost, locale, modality, reading type, and exact
provider/model/prompt/content/schema/safety versions. D-041 separately authorizes categorical
verified or safe-replacement outcomes. The durable interpretation model stores those facts, but
production AI remains safe-off and no owner dashboard or production aggregate reader is active.

RIT-038 must provide reusable operations metrics without creating a public/admin route, mixing
essential operations with consented product analytics, exposing private interpretation material,
or inventing production performance and monetary budgets.

## Decision

Adopt `ai-operations.v1` as a strict offline daily aggregate and private report contract.

- Use one exact 24-hour UTC window, captured no more than six hours after window end.
- Label the source as approved durable aggregate, synthetic fixture, or unavailable. A source
  becomes stale 30 hours after its observed-through time.
- Treat unavailable, stale, and synthetic sources as unusable: decision status is blocked,
  performance values are null, and only a source-refresh alert is permitted.
- Group only by locale, reading type, provider/model, prompt, output schema, safety policy, and
  approved content versions.
- Require at least 20 terminal generations before displaying overall or version-group values or
  opening a performance review.
- Report terminal volume, completion, failure, reviewed fallback, safe replacement, retry, average
  latency, latency over 30 seconds, cost/token reporting coverage, estimated cost, and average
  reported cost/tokens.
- Open human review when failure exceeds 2%, reviewed fallback exceeds 10%, safe replacement
  exceeds 5% of verified outcomes, more than 5% exceed 30 seconds, or cost/token reporting coverage
  falls below 95%.
- Treat reviewed fallbacks and safe replacements as protective outcomes. Threshold crossings do
  not authorize weakening safety, changing a model/provider, changing retries/timeouts, or
  modifying prompt/content.
- Keep monetary budget status unavailable until OWN-005 approves exact limits. Missing cost is
  never zero, and RIT-038 performs no automatic cost enforcement.
- Bind JSON/Markdown to the exact input SHA-256, write new mode-0600 files only, reject symlinks and
  oversized inputs, and never overwrite evidence.

Reject user, account, session, request, reading, order, or payment identifiers; questions,
prompts, outputs, fallback prose, retrieved excerpts, detailed safety/risk categories, raw
provider errors or responses, moderation payloads, journal/prayer/birth data, accessors, unknown
fields, and arbitrary metadata.

## Alternatives considered

| Option                         | Benefits                         | Costs/risks                                                 | Decision |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Add an admin dashboard now     | Visible UI                       | RIT-120 dependencies and admin activation are incomplete    | Rejected |
| Use optional product analytics | Existing taxonomy                | Wrong consent and privacy boundary for essential operations | Rejected |
| Store raw provider traces      | Maximum diagnosis                | Exposes private material and provider payloads              | Rejected |
| Invent a daily monetary budget | Enables spend alert              | OWN-005 has not approved a budget                           | Rejected |
| Offline aggregate metrics      | Private, deterministic, reusable | Real performance remains unavailable while AI is safe-off   | Selected |

## Consequences

CI verifies unavailable, stale, synthetic, low-sample, current, threshold, injection, accessor,
digest, symlink, and no-overwrite behavior without contacting a database, provider, or model. The
future RIT-120 dashboard can consume this report rather than reimplementing metric definitions.
RIT-127 remains responsible for approved budgets and automated cost controls.

The 30-second value is an operations review threshold, not a provider SLA or runtime timeout. Safe
replacement rate cannot be interpreted as a critical-safety-failure rate; fixed release evals
remain authoritative for critical safety.

## Validation

Pure analytics tests cover strict parsing, aggregate consistency, freshness, low-sample
suppression, metric math, threshold alerts, source blocking, accessor safety, and renderer
branding. CLI tests cover real byte digests, private file mode, exclusive output, and symlink
rejection. CI and build contracts require the dedicated verifier and compiled exports.

## Rollout and rollback

No database migration, production query, provider/model call, browser analytics, admin/public
route, user data, model or safety change, budget enforcement, deployment, DNS, or public launch is
added. Rollback removes the analytics module, report CLI, verification gate, and records. Local
private reports are not uploaded or committed automatically.
