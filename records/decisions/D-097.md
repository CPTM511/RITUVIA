# D-097: Approve the free English beta profile, intake-first entry, and direct-sale model

- Date: 2026-07-31
- Owners: owner, product, frontend, payments_risk, qa_security
- Related tasks: RIT-120, RIT-130, RIT-140, RIT-166, RIT-167, RIT-168, OWN-018
- Evidence references: explicit Owner approval in the 2026-07-31 Codex task; `AGENTS.md`;
  `docs/26_PRE_LAUNCH_EXECUTION_PLAN_ZH.md`; `docs/27_DETAILED_PRODUCT_USER_MANUAL_ZH.md`

## Context

The repository has a verified local anonymous reflection loop, but public production, paid
production, and even a protected external beta remain gated. The current homepage sends `Begin a
free reading` directly to one-card Tarot even though the approved core journey begins with a
question or theme and an existing deterministic safety intake. The root payment rule requires
direct sale of a named subscription, report, or digital experience without required Credit
preload, while the 2026-07-23 production pack describes Plus, prepaid Credit packs, and
Credit-priced Deep Readings.

The Owner explicitly approved all three recommended resolutions on 2026-07-31.

## Decision

Adopt the nearest external release target as a protected English closed beta for invited adults.
The beta is anonymous and free, uses an allowlisted noindex staging environment, and exposes only
the reflection core loop. It excludes account-required value, real payment, Credits,
subscriptions, production AI, astrology activation, real email delivery, cryptocurrency, public
indexing, and marketing activation.

Change the primary homepage free-reading entry so the user passes through the approved safety
intake before one-card Tarot. The intake remains deterministic, private, fail-closed, and subject
to its activation-reference contract. Crisis and blocked states never offer a continue-to-reading
action. This decision approves implementation and verification, not deployment or public
activation.

For future paid production offers, sell clearly named reports, subscriptions, or digital
experiences directly. Do not require users to preload Credits. Freeze prepaid Credit packs and
Credit-priced consumption as production product contracts. Existing Test Mode Credit, ledger,
subscription, refund, replay, and reconciliation implementation remains retained evidence until a
separate dependency audit proves what can be safely retired; it does not define the production
offer.

## Alternatives considered

| Option                                              | Benefits                                                           | Costs/risks                                                                                        | Decision |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Launch the complete production-pack surface at once | Preserves every planned destination                                | Expands safety, legal, payment, support, localization, and recovery risk before product validation | Rejected |
| Protected free English beta first                   | Tests comprehension and trust with the smallest reversible surface | Defers paid and advanced capabilities                                                              | Selected |
| Keep homepage direct-to-Tarot                       | Shorter click path                                                 | Bypasses the approved question/theme safety and agency step                                        | Rejected |
| Route homepage through safety intake                | Makes the visible journey match the approved core contract         | Requires navigation and browser-evidence changes                                                   | Selected |
| Require prepaid Credits                             | Reuses the existing Test Mode commercial kernel                    | Conflicts with the higher-priority direct-sale/no-preload rule                                     | Rejected |
| Directly sell named experiences                     | Matches root authority and is simpler for users                    | Exact SKUs, prices, refunds, provider underwriting, and legal review remain open                   | Selected |

## Consequences

RIT-166 will implement and verify the intake-first homepage entry after RIT-164 closes the current
recovery blocker. RIT-130 will prepare only the approved protected English anonymous free-beta
profile. Excluded capabilities must be absent from beta navigation and remain server-side safe-off.

OWN-018 remains blocked, but its unresolved question is narrowed: the product model is now direct
sale without required Credit preload; the remaining work is an exact provider/legal-reviewed SKU,
entitlement, price, refund, and customer-language contract. OWN-002, OWN-004, OWN-005, RIT-140, and
all production payment and launch gates remain unchanged.

This decision does not approve a price, country, legal policy, payment provider, merchant category,
tax treatment, production identity provider, production AI provider, deployment, DNS change,
public indexing, data migration, or real-value transaction.

## Validation

The homepage browser journey must begin at `/en`, enter the activated `/en/intake` route, prove
allowed, reframed, blocked, crisis, offline, unavailable, and retry behavior, and continue to the
same deterministic one-card reading without persisting question text. The full anonymous loop must
continue to pass without account creation, payment, production AI, or private-data leakage.

Beta release evidence must prove allowlist protection, noindex behavior, staging isolation,
invitation/consent boundaries, support ownership, rollback, and all applicable security,
accessibility, recovery, and operations gates.

## Rollout and rollback

Rollout first changes documentation and backlog authority, then implements RIT-166 as one bounded
navigation/browser slice. Existing direct-to-Tarot routes remain valid destinations; only the
primary free-reading entry changes. Rollback restores the homepage link while leaving the intake
route and this historical decision record intact, and records why the approved intake-first path
could not be retained.

Freezing prepaid Credits requires no destructive deletion. Existing Test Mode implementation stays
safe-off and auditable until a separate evidence-bound retirement task establishes callers,
historical replay, privacy, payment, migration, audit, and rollback obligations.
