## Context

Swiss Ephemeris supplies deterministic astronomical calculations, but it does not choose the
RITUVIA product method. Zodiac, node, body list, house system, aspects, orbs, uncertainty
publication, and polar failure behavior must be explicit and versioned so calculations remain
replayable and the product does not silently mix conventions.

RIT-093 proposed three bounded methods. Option A preserves the approved M9 natal scope while
failing closed whenever exact houses cannot be calculated and avoiding misleading detail for
approximate or unknown birth times.

## Decision

Approve OWN-015 Option A as `rituvia-western-natal.v1`:

- use the tropical zodiac;
- calculate Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, and the True
  Node;
- use Placidus houses only when birth time is exact;
- calculate conjunction and opposition with an inclusive 8 degree orb, trine and square with an
  inclusive 6 degree orb, and sextile with an inclusive 4 degree orb;
- for approximate birth time, calculate planetary positions at the supplied center instant,
  disclose the exact approximation window, and suppress houses, angles, and aspects;
- for unknown birth time, publish no placements, houses, angles, or aspects and do not invoke the
  ephemeris;
- when Placidus houses or angles fail, return unavailable without partial placements, without a
  different house system, and without automatic fallback; and
- require tropical, True Node, Placidus, body-list, aspect-policy, engine, source/data, adapter,
  compiler, ABI, and SBOM versions in every persisted calculation.

These are RITUVIA product conventions for symbolic reflection. They are not universal astrology
rules, scientific personality claims, predictions, diagnoses, or professional advice.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Benefits | Costs/risks | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A: Tropical and Placidus conservative V1 | Completes the intended natal facts while preserving strict uncertainty and failure behavior | Placidus can be unavailable at polar latitudes | Selected |
| B: Tropical and Whole Sign V1 | Avoids many polar house failures | Materially changes the approved house convention | Not selected |
| C: Positions-only V1 | Minimizes method assumptions | Does not complete the M9 natal-house scope | Not selected |

## Consequences

RIT-093 may replace provisional constants with the checksummed method catalog and implement
authorized, encrypted, immutable calculation persistence. Historical calculations must retain this
exact method and cannot be silently recomputed under a successor.

Approximate-time UI must show the approximation window and cannot render house or aspect
inferences. Unknown-time UI cannot imply a noon chart or derive any placement. Polar failure must
offer correction/retry guidance without substituting another house system.

This approval does not activate production astrology, publish an API or UI, approve public
deployment, change legal/privacy text, or waive reference-vector, privacy, security, source,
Corresponding Source, or staging kill-switch gates.

## Validation

Focused tests must verify the catalog digest, all body/aspect/orb constants, inclusive orb
boundaries, exact/approximate/unknown suppression, house failure without partial facts, engine-flag
attestation, persisted version provenance, idempotent replay, ownership, privacy export, and
crypto-shred deletion.

## Rollout and rollback

The method remains server-side and safe-off until RIT-093 release evidence passes. Rollback stops
new calculations and retains immutable encrypted historical rows. A method change requires a new
catalog version and successor decision; it must never rewrite existing facts.
