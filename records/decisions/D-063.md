## Context

RIT-044 intentionally stores local calendar date, IANA time zone, and quiet hours while fixing
reminder preference to `none` and channel to null. Activating those fields would silently change a
published contract and could confuse scheduling data with delivery consent. Account analytics,
personalization, and model-improvement consent are also different purposes and cannot authorize a
service notification. Browser-local state or a mutable provider job cannot prove immediate
withdrawal, owner isolation, retries, deletion, or once-only delivery.

## Decision

Add a separate account-owned `revisit-reminder-preference.v1` aggregate for one English email sent
at most once for one scheduled Revisit. Require exact `rituvia.revisit-reminder-notice.v1`,
`email`, `once`, and explicit subscribe or unsubscribe. Preserve stable idempotency in an
append-only operation ledger and keep the current queue state in one additive subscription row.

Use PostgreSQL database time, local date/time zone, a 09:00 local threshold, stored quiet hours,
`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, hashed 60-second leases, three attempts, deterministic bounded backoff,
and terminal dead-letter state. Recheck active account, ownership link, Revisit/intention
lifecycle, recipient identity, preference, locale, contract versions, quiet hours, and lease
immediately before provider invocation. Committed unsubscribe or privacy deletion clears a live
lease.

Keep job/provider input to identifiers, exact versions, and fixed lock-screen-safe copy. The
subject, preview, body, and private-route action cannot include question, intention, small action,
ritual, journal, relationship, health, or generated interpretation details. Use the subscription
ID as the stable provider idempotency key. Compose only a disabled provider adapter; no recipient
decryption, vendor SDK, network call, production scheduler, or send activation is approved.

## Alternatives considered

| Option                                        | Benefits             | Costs/risks                                                             | Why not selected                                 |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Change Revisit v1 reminder fields             | Fewer tables         | Breaks exact replay and turns inert policy into consent                 | Keep the published lifecycle contract unchanged  |
| Reuse analytics or AI consent                 | Fewer controls       | Bundles materially different purposes                                   | Service notifications require explicit authority |
| Browser timer/local storage                   | Fast prototype       | No durable ownership, withdrawal, retry, or once-only proof             | Delivery authority must be server-side           |
| Store rendered email and address in the queue | Simple provider call | Duplicates sensitive data and expands breach/log surface                | Resolve only inside a later approved adapter     |
| Activate a provider now                       | Real external send   | Missing approved domain, copy, credentials, monitoring, and launch gate | Keep production delivery safe-off                |

## Consequences

Signed-in users can explicitly enable or disable one reminder and see only committed state.
Anonymous users keep the complete Revisit flow and receive a non-error empty reminder discovery
response. A claimed row is displayed publicly as pending rather than exposing lease internals.
Delivered rows are immutable through the preference API; subsequent opt-out belongs to future
general preference-center policy.

Privacy export includes visible preference, delivery, provider reference, and operation evidence
but excludes hashes and lease material. Privacy deletion cancels pending, retrying, leased,
delivered, or dead-lettered rows before identity tombstoning and preserves append-only operation
history under the existing evidence-retention policy.

## Rollout and rollback

The migration is additive. Rollback removes Web and Worker composition and stops new claims while
retaining subscription and operation rows. Do not delete or rewrite evidence. Activating a
production email provider/domain, recipient resolution, final consent/unsubscribe copy, another
locale, scheduler deployment, delivery metrics/alerts, retention changes, or public sending
requires a later reviewed decision and explicit owner approval.

## Validation

Focused Domain, Worker, route/server/proxy/message tests cover strict contracts, fixed copy,
send-time authorization, provider timeout/retry/dead-letter, anonymous safe state, CSRF,
idempotency, and safe errors. Isolated PostgreSQL evidence covers all 25 migrations, owner
isolation, replay/conflict, quiet hours, concurrent claim, lease fencing, immediate withdrawal,
once-only delivery, private canaries, and least privilege. Privacy export/deletion, affected
package builds, architecture, Revisit browser, continuous anonymous loop, and the production
artifact accessibility boundary pass.

The owner requested continued autonomous local implementation. This decision does not approve
