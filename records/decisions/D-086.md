# D-086: Conservative visible GEO answer authority projection

- Date: 2026-07-29
- Owners: product, growth_seo, content, localization, qa_security
- Related tasks: RIT-116
- Evidence references: `content/editorial/geo-answer-context.en.v1.json`;
  `apps/web/server/geo-answer-context.ts`;
  `apps/web/app/_components/geo-answer-context.tsx`;
  `scripts/web-shell-build-policy.mjs`;
  `scripts/verify-public-search-browser.mjs`

## Context

The approved 45-page English public inventory already binds every route to a unique intent,
source digest, review window, canonical path, content shape, and internal-link hierarchy. The
pages also lead with a direct human-readable answer. Their visible source and review treatment,
however, differs by family: core pages have no source block, numerology review is embedded in
prose, astrology includes a hard-coded deployment-oriented status, and Tarot and ritual guide
pages omit the review metadata shown on their hubs. No page visibly names and defines one stable
subject entity while separating fact or method, tradition, symbolic interpretation, and product
policy.

Adding richer JSON-LD would not solve the human-facing inconsistency and would conflict with
D-084 unless every claim first had visible, truthful authority. Internal source paths, hashes,
locators, author IDs, and reviewer IDs are integrity evidence rather than reader-facing content.
Owner review also must not be presented as independent, institutional, religious, scientific, or
expert endorsement.

## Decision

Use one conservative server-rendered answer-authority projection across the exact current
45-page inventory. Keep the existing answer near the top of each page, then display one semantic
section containing:

- one stable entity name and definition;
- one to three explicit classifications from the closed
  `fact | tradition | interpretation | product_guidance` vocabulary;
- approved human-readable source titles and versions;
- the truthful authority type, reviewed date, and review-due date when one exists.

Store the English labels, entity definitions, and classification statements in the strict
checksummed `rituvia-geo-answer-context.v1` content record. Resolve each page only through the
current public-page inventory and route registry. A missing route, expired page authority,
expired GEO content review, unknown family, unsupported entity, unsafe text, duplicate source,
internal path, or markup injection fails closed.

The public label for `product_guidance` is “Product policy.” “Fact or documented method” is
limited to calculation or implementation-method statements that the approved sources support.
Western astrology conventions and Tarot symbolic vocabulary are labeled as tradition. Symbolic
meanings remain interpretation, not fact.

Keep the D-084 structured-data graph unchanged. Do not add author, publisher, reviewer, date,
FAQ, HowTo, Product, Offer, rating, review, or organization claims. Do not add routes, hidden
summaries, bot-only text, keyword blocks, analytics, personalization, another locale, another
tradition, deployment, or indexing activation.

## Alternatives considered

| Option                                      | Benefits                                         | Costs/risks                                               | Decision |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Expand JSON-LD first                        | More machine fields                              | Creates unsupported or invisible authority claims         | Rejected |
| Keep family-specific source blocks          | Smallest code diff                               | Preserves omissions and semantic drift across 45 pages    | Rejected |
| Publish internal editorial records directly | Maximum provenance detail                        | Exposes paths, IDs, hashes, and governance implementation | Rejected |
| Claim expert or institutional review        | Stronger marketing language                      | False authority and cultural-integrity risk               | Rejected |
| Shared visible conservative projection      | Human-useful, consistent, testable, source-bound | Adds a repeated compact context section                   | Selected |

## Consequences

The exact 45 pages now share one visible contract while retaining distinct answers and substantive
content. The public-page quality source includes the GEO record in every source-set digest and
visible-content digest. Decision-backed core pages may bind reviewed `apps/web/` sources plus
strict `content/editorial/` sources; other arbitrary content paths remain rejected.

The GEO content record is publishable for public display but is not independently indexable and
owns no canonical URL. Its review expires on 2027-07-29 unless a reviewed replacement is
registered. The existing route authorities retain their own earlier review dates and due dates.

## Validation

Pure tests exhaust all 45 route records, five stable entities, all four classifications, current
review windows, unsafe markup, internal paths, duplicate sources, and stale authority. Static
component tests cover all hubs and every guide through the existing family loops. The 45-artifact
build policy requires exactly one visible semantic section and rejects hidden content,
unsupported classifications, internal evidence, unknown entities, or duplicate sections.

The existing public search Chromium gate adds the astrology shape, 320 px reflow, visible-content
checks, no-JavaScript rendering, serious/critical Axe checks, private canaries, same-origin network
containment, and continued minimal JSON-LD validation without adding another CI workflow.

## Rollout and rollback

No database, migration, provider, analytics, user data, route, country, locale, DNS, deployment,
Search Console action, actual indexing, or public launch is added. Rollback removes the shared
component and strict GEO content record, restores the previous family source sections, rebuilds
the public-page inventory, and removes the GEO artifact assertions. No persisted or external state
requires reversal.
