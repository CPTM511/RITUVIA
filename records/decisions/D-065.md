# D-065: Approved English numerology interpretation and publication pack

- Date: 2026-07-25
- Owners: owner, product, content, ai_safety, growth_seo, payments_risk
- Related tasks: OWN-012, RIT-083, RIT-084
- Evidence references: owner reply `批准英文数秘解释内容、来源权利、审阅者及付费产品映射，并继续RIT-084`; `content/traditions/numerology/rituvia-symbolic-reflection.en.v1.json`

## Context

RIT-083 had already implemented a provider-neutral, safe-off numerology interpretation boundary
and fixed synthetic evaluations, but it could not use synthetic fixtures as production content.
RIT-084 also required useful public education pages with reviewed copy, rights, source notes,
canonical metadata, structured data, and strict non-predictive boundaries. OWN-012 therefore
blocked approval of the exact English corpus, rights inventory, reviewer records, prompt,
fallback, and paid-product mapping.

## Decision

Approve the English publication catalog
`rituvia.numerology.symbolic-reflection.en` version `1.0.0`, whose reviewed file SHA-256 is
`6b4da935eb3c9fd8e62a9a2acff4c3acd6008139e434f51999ec9e18deda25f0`.

The approval covers:

- thirty-six unique interpretation entries for Life Path, Birthday Number, and Personal Year
  across `1` through `9`, `11`, `22`, and `33`;
- the exact English prompt `rituvia.numerology.prompt.en` version `1.0.0`, fallback
  `rituvia.numerology.fallback.en` version `1.0.0`, and independent semantic-review registration
  `rituvia.numerology.semantic-reviewer.en` version `1.0.0` under policy
  `numerology-interpretation-safety.en.v1`;
- the two RITUVIA-owned source records and their worldwide public-display, commercial, AI,
  SEO-publication, and translation uses;
- public publication of `/en/numerology` plus substantive Life Path, Birthday Number, Personal
  Year, and master-number guides;
- keeping all number-profile records non-indexed and unavailable as public doorway pages; and
- optional verified numerology context for product `year_reflection`, fulfillment
  `deep_reading.year_reflection`, priced at six Credits, with activation remaining `safe_off`.

The exact RITUVIA V1 arithmetic remains governed by D-064. Public and generated copy must identify
the method as a RITUVIA symbolic-reflection convention, not a scientific method, identity
statement, prediction, diagnosis, professional advice, or paid spiritual efficacy claim.

## Consequences

OWN-012, RIT-083, and RIT-084 may be marked `Done` for repository implementation. The approved
corpus can be projected through the existing integrity- and authority-checked AI boundary, and the
five exact public education routes can be indexed in production environments.

This decision does not activate a production AI provider/model, semantic-review service, API,
personalized numerology UI, database persistence, Credits reservation or consumption, paid
fulfillment, another locale, deployment, DNS change, or public launch. Those remain behind their
existing implementation and owner gates.

## Validation

The catalog parser must fail closed on approval, source, rights, publication, profile inventory,
prompt, fallback, reviewer, and paid-product drift. The projected artifacts must pass the RIT-083
integrity, authority, deterministic-fact, safety, and complete-inventory contract. Public routes
must be exact allowlisted static documents with unique metadata, canonical/hreflang, useful
server-rendered content, JSON-LD, internal links, mobile layout, accessibility checks, no private
input, and no profile doorway routes.

## Rollout and rollback

Repository rollout is safe-off AI artifact projection plus static English education publication.
Production deployment remains separately gated. Before production activation, rollback removes
the five public route records and approved artifact projection while retaining D-064 arithmetic
and the private deterministic calculator. After any future paid report is persisted, rollback must
preserve its exact content, prompt, fallback, source, calculation, product, and policy versions.
