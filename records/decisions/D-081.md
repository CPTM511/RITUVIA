## Decision

Approve the exact candidate content digest for RITUVIA-owned English public display, commercial
use, SEO publication, and future reviewed translation. Record the owner as reviewer on 2026-07-28,
with review due 2027-07-28.

Promote only the deterministic approval-envelope fields needed to bind that reviewed content to
this decision. The resulting approved artifact SHA-256 is
`15a1d9d5f5502ddf8c14543da06bc85813dc0b67cd7dae1cf9a0044ba72b8595`; no title, explanation,
source claim, card route, spread route, or safety text changed during promotion.

Activate exactly 25 English educational routes:

- one `/en/tarot` library hub;
- 22 declared Major Arcana card routes;
- `/en/tarot/one-card-spread`;
- `/en/tarot/situation-action-possibility-spread`.

These routes become indexable only when the existing public-shell crawl gate is enabled in a
production environment. Keep AI retrieval, personalized-result indexing, orientation/theme/profile
doorway routes, new traditions, new locales, deployments, DNS changes, and public launch excluded.

The source catalog remains a RITUVIA original symbolic-reflection adaptation using a declared
Rider-Waite-Smith ordering convention. It is not represented as independent historical,
scientific, religious, divinatory, or institutional authority, and no traditional deck artwork is
published.

## Consequences

The approved artifact enters the shared editorial registry and the 25 routes enter the localized
public route registry, canonical metadata, production-only robots allowlist, and English Tarot
sitemap. Private reading routes remain private and noindex. Search indexing still cannot occur in
local, preview, or staging environments, or while the production public shell is disabled.

Future body-copy, route, rights, source, review, locale, or publication-scope changes require a new
reviewed artifact and authority update. Future translations require their own source-bound human
review and locale activation.

## Validation

Focused contract tests must pin both the reviewed candidate digest and final approved artifact
digest, reject forged approval or route/source drift, and prove the exact route and sitemap
inventory. Production-build and browser checks must cover representative hub, card, and spread
pages for canonical metadata, structured data, responsive layout, accessibility, console/network
errors, and production-only indexing.

## Rollout and rollback

No database migration, AI provider, payment, country, locale, deployment, DNS, or public-launch
action is authorized. Roll back by removing the Tarot definitions from the public registry and
shared editorial registry, returning metadata to noindex, and retaining the source and decision
records for audit.
