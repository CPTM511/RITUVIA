# D-075: Test-only RTL pseudolocale and structural bidi boundary

- Date: 2026-07-27
- Owners: owner, product, localization, frontend, qa_security
- Related tasks: RIT-101, RIT-103, RIT-104, RIT-115
- Evidence references: `packages/i18n/src/pseudolocale.ts`; `scripts/rtl-policy.ts`;
  `apps/web/test/rtl-contract.test.ts`

## Context

RITUVIA needs RTL-safe architecture before selecting or launching a reviewed Arabic locale.
Previously, Web browser smoke tests applied a local accent transform after English rendered, the
direction resolver recognized only four hard-coded language subtags, and current technical and
user-authored values were not consistently isolated from surrounding text direction.

RIT-101 also names email and share cards even though localized email delivery belongs to RIT-104
and redacted localized share cards belong to RIT-115. No Arabic catalog, route, email template, or
share-card implementation is approved.

## Decision

Adopt `en-XA` and `ar-XB` as test-only pseudolocales. They are never runtime-supported locales and
cannot enter public routes, canonical metadata, hreflang, sitemap, reminder delivery, or share
output. Pseudolocalization transforms ICU literal nodes only, preserves arguments, plural/select
branches, tags, URLs, email addresses, local routes, and the RITUVIA brand, expands transformable
copy by at least forty percent, and rejects invisible bidi controls.

Resolve text direction from canonical `Intl.Locale` metadata rather than a hand-maintained
language list. Production CSS uses logical directional properties; back/forward icons mirror in
RTL, while mathematical formulas, dates, times, engine identifiers, and the astrology wheel stay
explicitly LTR. User-authored text and locale-formatted prices/timestamps use structural
`dir="auto"` or a `bdi` element rather than translation-embedded bidi controls.

The current English reminder remains plain text and `revisit-reminder.en.v1`; RIT-101 exercises its
copy through both test pseudolocales without authorizing an Arabic template. Current private
product pages remain noindex and emit no Open Graph or Twitter share output. RIT-104 and RIT-115
must implement and verify their actual localized outputs before those capabilities can claim RTL
completion.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Benefits | Costs/risks | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Activate `/ar` with machine copy | End-to-end route realism | Violates owner, review, support, and cultural-safety gates | Rejected |
| Keep post-render text mutation only | Minimal code | Misses ICU structure, direction metadata, and production import leakage | Rejected |
| Embed FSI/PDI controls in translations | Local string-level isolation | Invisible controls are difficult to audit and can be abused | Rejected |
| Test-only ICU pseudolocales plus structural isolation | Broad architecture evidence without false launch | Real Arabic still requires reviewed content and route work | Selected |

## Consequences

The RTL repository gate rejects physical left/right CSS, invisible bidi controls, production
pseudolocale imports, and loss of reviewed icon, form, chart, or technical-value contracts. The
production accessibility inventory now includes all approved numerology and astrology education
routes, and the shell verifier admits only their previously owner-approved, exact JSON-LD shape.

The root document remains correctly English because `en` is still the only runtime locale.
RIT-103 must bind future reviewed locale routes to SSR document language/direction when such a
locale is actually admitted. Test-only browser mutation is evidence for layout behavior, not a
claim that Arabic SSR or hydration is production-ready.

## Validation

Focused unit coverage verifies RTL script metadata, strict pseudolocale admission, ICU structural
equivalence across the current Web message inventory, protected technical values, reminder-copy
boundaries, private social-output denial, logical CSS, and structural isolation.

Production-artifact Chromium covers fourteen public and three private routes with expanded LTR and
RTL states, keyboard focus, touch targets, dark/reduced-motion/no-JavaScript behavior, local-only
requests, and exact reviewed Axe incomplete targets. Dedicated numerology, astrology, and Revisit
flows verify 320px RTL reflow; astrology additionally verifies 400% zoom, forced colors, and an
LTR wheel.

## Rollout and rollback

No locale, country, translation, email provider, share output, production deployment, DNS, payment,
customer data, migration, or external service changes. Rollback removes the test-only package
export, RTL gate, structural isolation markup, and expanded browser inventory. It does not require
data reversal.
