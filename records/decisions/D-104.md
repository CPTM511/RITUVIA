## Context

D-097 approved the nearest external target as a protected English anonymous free closed Beta.
D-100 and RIT-122 provide one privacy-minimal per-session intake/mutation budget, but deliberately
left production thresholds, invite/cohort assumptions, protected ingress, observation, and rollback
behind OWN-019. The Owner reviewed the exact 2026-08-02 decision request and approved it without
changes.

## Decision

Approve `own-019.protected-beta-abuse.v1` for the protected English anonymous free closed-Beta
candidate with this exact profile:

- maximum 25 invited adults; English only;
- deny-by-default admission through individually revocable single-use invites; no public signup;
- protected standing-staging allowlist plus managed bot/rate protection; the application stores no
  IP address, User-Agent, device identifier, or fingerprint;
- at most 30 new anonymous sessions per 60 seconds across the protected deployment;
- at most 12 question-intake checks per active session per 60 seconds;
- at most 120 protected mutations per active session per 86,400 seconds;
- no automatic retry after `429`; show the bounded calm pause and `Retry-After` recovery;
- a 72-hour staff dry run followed by seven days of daily Owner review;
- immediate rollback for unauthorized access, private-data leakage, safety-boundary bypass, data
  loss/corruption, any open Critical/High finding, or inability to revoke admission;
- SLO rollback for a critical Beta SLO breach not contained within its runbook window;
- abuse rollback for repeated invite bypass/session farming or legitimate-user rate-limit impact
  above 10% in a one-hour reviewed sample;
- rollback stops invitations, applies reviewed Web `read_only` containment where safe, revokes edge
  admission, preserves evidence, and requires Owner re-approval before resumption.

This approval authorizes RIT-130 to encode and verify the exact profile in protected standing
staging after its release-sequence dependencies are complete. It does not select an edge provider,
install credentials, deploy, change DNS, activate public access, or approve launch.

## Consequences

OWN-019 is Done. RIT-130's Owner-policy dependency is satisfied, but RIT-130 remains Planned while
RIT-127 is the sole Ready task and RIT-128 is the next operations/security exercise. Protected
standing staging, invite implementation, provider-level restore, external DAST/penetration testing,
Gate H, the RIT-130 evidence bundle, and a separate deployment decision remain incomplete.

Production configuration must use the exact policy reference and values above as one fail-closed
unit. Missing, partial, or drifted configuration must deny protected intake/mutations rather than
fall back to local defaults. Local acceptance policy names remain local-only evidence.

## Validation

RIT-130 must prove exact configuration matching, deny-by-default admission, single-use/revocable/
expiring invites, cohort cap, edge/app layering, normal and `429` browser recovery, no private
canary leakage, monitoring, rollback, and independent-security evidence in standing staging. The
final evidence bundle still requires Owner review before any deployment action.

## Rollout and rollback

This record changes approval state only. It performs no rollout. If later evidence invalidates the
profile, stop invitations and ingress, preserve evidence, mark the release task blocked, and create
a superseding Owner decision; do not silently change limits or reuse the approval for public/paid
production.
