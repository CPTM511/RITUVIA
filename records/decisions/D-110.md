# D-110: Compose exact payment route controls and forbid provider fallback

- Date: 2026-08-03
- Owners: operations, payments_risk, qa_security
- Related tasks: RIT-075, RIT-074, RIT-141
- Evidence references: `docs/runbooks/RIT-075_PAYMENT_PROVIDER_CONTROLS.md`;
  `packages/payments/test/payment-route-control.test.ts`; D-097

## Context

Country Policy and three default-off feature flags already existed, but the two new-purchase
services did not consume one shared fail-closed decision before persistence, provider invocation,
or return of an already attached Checkout URL. Automatic provider failover was also not explicitly
forbidden by a typed contract.

## Decision

Every new fiat or crypto purchase route must compose the exact country activation flag, the exact
checkout-kind flag, and one Country Policy decision at the same server-owned instant. Authorization
is bound to country, currency/asset, provider, method, payment kind, recurrence, registry version,
flag versions, and policy version.

The caller must pass an empty fallback-provider list. Any non-empty list, malformed evidence,
scope/time/version mismatch, checkout safe-off state, or policy mismatch fails closed. No alternate
provider or method may be selected automatically.

Apply this control before creating an order, reserving a subscription, calling a provider, or
returning an existing Checkout URL. Do not apply it to signed webhooks, refunds, disputes,
fulfillment, entitlement restoration, or reconciliation for already-created obligations.

## Alternatives considered

Provider-local switches, client-visible switches, automatic fallback, and gating settlement were
rejected. They either fragment authorization, expose mutable client state, cross an unapproved
provider route, or prevent existing customer obligations from completing safely.

## Consequences

New purchase issuance is fail-closed and exact-route only. Operational control-plane faults and
checkout safe-off return a redacted unavailable response; country/provider/method denials remain a
calm eligibility denial. A URL copied before safe-off cannot be revoked by this repository, and
existing subscription renewals may still emit signed provider events that must be processed.

## Validation

Focused contract, configuration, service, replay, subscription, and HTTP tests cover independent
country/checkout controls, forged activation evidence, provider/method substitution, non-empty
fallback, control-reader failure, old-URL replay, and redacted response mapping.

## Sources and approvals

- Root `AGENTS.md` payment, Country Policy, privacy, least-privilege, and human-approval rules.
- D-094 command/provider separation, D-097 direct-sale/no-preload direction, and D-101 settlement-
  preserving read-only containment.
- Production-pack provider kill-switch, no-new-work, pending-recovery, and release-gate controls.
- No provider, credential, paid product, deployment, or production authority is granted.

## Rollout and rollback

No provider, credential, network call, deployment, pricing, paid UI, or production activation is
part of this decision. Rollback removes the pure contract, Web reader/composition, service guards,
tests, and records; no database or external state requires reversal.
