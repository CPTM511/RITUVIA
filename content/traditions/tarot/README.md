# Tarot content fixtures

`rituvia-placeholder.v1.json` is an internal, synthetic, English-only contract fixture. It is not a
historical tarot tradition, a production deck, approved spiritual content, publishable copy,
indexable material, or AI-retrieval input. Its three original neutral card identities exist only to
validate the one-card and ordered three-card schemas without copying artwork or interpretive text.

Text and artwork rights remain separate. The fixture has no artwork, allows only internal
validation, and must fail the publication eligibility assessment. A real deck requires independently
verified text and artwork rights, sources, attribution, qualified editorial review, complete theme
coverage, and the applicable owner gates.
